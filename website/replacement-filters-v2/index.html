Simple 5-minute replacement

No plumber required. The filter set is designed for straightforward DIY replacement using your existing Neptune removal tool.

1
Turn off the mains water supply at your Neptune shut-off valve
2
Use your Neptune removal tool to unscrew each filter housing
3
Remove old filters, rinse housings, and insert new filters
4
Tighten housings securely and restore mains water supply
5
Run water briefly to flush the system — done
