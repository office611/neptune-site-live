Part 2 · The Zoom call

Hard rules. Every question asked with full confidence. Feelings before features. Never promise a health outcome ("it'll fix your eczema"); say what customers tell us. Never say the system is built for Melbourne's water; say it suits it and the set-up is tailored. No fake urgency.
1
Open

Warm, then set the plan

Hey [Name], great to finally put a face to it. How's your day going?
Here's how this'll go: I'll ask a few questions about your home and what's going on, show you what we'd set up for you, and give you your price. If it feels right, we'll lock in your install date today. Sound good?

Getting a "yes" to the plan up front makes the ask at the end feel expected, not pushy. If a decision maker isn't on, ask now: "Is anyone else part of this decision? Let's grab them."

2
Their why

Get to the feeling

Ask, then shut up and listen. Use their words back to them later.

  • What made you reach out about your water?
  • How long has that been going on?
  • Who in the house is it affecting most? (Often a partner or kid, not the person on the call.)
  • What have you tried so far? How did that go?
  • How does that make you feel, watching them deal with it?
  • If this was sorted, what would that look like day to day?
  • Why now? What made today the day?
So what I'm hearing is [their words]. You've tried [X], it hasn't helped, and you want [their day-to-day picture]. Is that right?

The summary is the most important line on the call. When they say "exactly", they've sold themselves.

The filters · tell it through the materials

3 filters, 4 stages. Talk about what they're made of and how, not a list of what they remove. The craft sells it.

The GuardStage 1 · 5 micron · pleated sediment

Pleated polypropylene, folded over and over to pack in as much surface area as possible. It's the first thing your water meets: it catches the grit, rust and sand from the main so everything after it stays clean.

The DisruptorStages 2 + 3 · 1 micron · scale-reduction carbon block

A dense carbon block infused with polyphosphate crystals. As water passes through, the crystals stop hard minerals from crystallising into scale on your glass, taps and appliances, without taking the minerals out of your water. Packed tight so your pressure stays strong.

The ExtractorStage 4 · 1 micron · coconut shell activated carbon

Made from coconut shells, charred at high heat, then activated with steam to open up millions of microscopic pores. A single gram has more than 1,000 m² of surface area inside it, nearly four tennis courts. Then it's compressed into a dense block. The cheap filters use coal-based carbon. This is the good stuff.

Only if they ask for numbers: reduces up to 99% of chlorine and up to 85% of heavy metals. WaterMark certified. Filters changed every 12 months ($300 a set, delivered). The chlorine test tablet shows any pink when it's time.

Referral vs discount

If they push for a discount, you can give one, but discounted orders aren't part of the referral program. Let them choose. Most will keep the referral, because it's worth more.

I can take $200 off for you. Just so you know, discounted orders aren't part of our referral program. You mentioned 3 people who'd want this, which is $1,500 you'd get back. Totally up to you which you'd rather.

Only say this if it's true for everyone: the terms page says referral rewards aren't available on discounted purchases. Never invent a "usual price" or a "today only" deal.

Objections

"I need to think about it."

Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think through? If it's something I can answer now, let's sort it.

If they can't name it, go back to their why ("You said [their words]…") and ask again.

"I need to talk to my partner."

Of course. Can we grab them now for five minutes? Otherwise let's book a time today when you're both on, so you're not relaying it second-hand.

"It's expensive."

Compared to what? Over 10 years it's under $2 a day for the whole house, and you've got 3 people who'd want it, which is $1,500 back.

"Can someone come out and look first?"

That's the one thing we don't do, on purpose. Your photos go to our plumbing team, who plan it before they arrive. That's what keeps it quick and keeps your price where it is.

"How do I know it works?"

On install day we test your water in front of you, before and after. And if a test shows chlorine in your filtered water in the first 30 days, we'll check it and make it right.

"Someone else quoted cheaper."

Plenty of them use the cheapest coal-based carbon and a generic set-up. Yours is tailored to your home, installed by licensed plumbers, with a 7-year workmanship warranty. What did they include?
