Call 1 Script

The first call. Ring about 3 minutes after the survey, while they're reading the info page. Book the Zoom, get the photos coming. No price on this call.

The call If they ask After the call Rules

The call

1
Open

Warm and quick

Hey, is this [Name]? It's [your name] from Neptune Filtration — how you going?
2
Where they're at

Their story, briefly

So tell me — what’s actually been going on with your water?

Then stop and let them talk. Don’t fill the silence: when they stop, wait 2 seconds. The real reason often comes after the pause.

Join in when they’ve told you what it is and who it affects, or they start repeating themselves. Say their words back, then ask the next one:

So it’s [their words, e.g. the eczema in the shower]. That makes sense.
How long’s it been bothering you?
Have you tried anything already to help with it?

If they mention jugs, bottled water or shower filters:

Roughly what are you spending on that?

Then:

And how many of you are at home?

One question at a time. Short answer like “just want better water”? One follow-up (“anything you’ve noticed, taste, smell, skin?”), then move on. Going on and on? “That’s exactly what your specialist will dig into on the Zoom.” The whole step is 1–2 minutes.

Don’t ask their suburb, the survey already has it.

3
Read check

Did they read the info?

So when you read through the info after the survey, what specifically did you like about it?

They name something: link it to their problem in one line, then move on

Yeah, [what they liked] is a big one. That's exactly what's going on with [their problem, e.g. the eczema in the shower].

Examples. Chlorine test: “That's the one most people can't believe, and it's in your shower every day.” A customer video: “Cecilio's one gets everyone, same as what you're dealing with.” No home visit: “That's exactly why we do it this way, no one at your kitchen table.” Put what they liked in your notes, it's your opener on the Zoom.

They didn't read it:

No stress, I'll text you the link after our call.
4
We can help → photos

Photos before price

Based on what you've told me, our system will definitely help.

Pause. Let them react ("oh good"). That's their first yes.

All our customers send us a few photos first, so your price is exact, not a guess.

Skin or hair: never promise the condition goes away. Let the customer videos say that.

5
Two options

Zoom last, so it gets picked

From there, you've got two options. One of our filtration specialists can come out to your home: that's $200, and if you go ahead we take it off the price. Or you jump on a quick Zoom with one of them, and we can get you in today. Most people go with the Zoom. Which suits you better?

Then stop talking until they answer. Don't add anything to fill the gap.

6
The offer + decision makers

Now the 20%, then everyone on

Perfect. Right now we're running 20% off when you go ahead at your appointment, and we've got 0% interest options too. On that, is this a decision you're making with anyone else — partner, wife?

Say it straight through, then pause and wait for their answer. A pause after the 20% invites “20% off what?”. If they ask anyway: “Your specialist will take you through your price on the Zoom, that's why we want everyone there.”

Someone else is involved:

Let's get them on the Zoom too, so you and [your partner / their name] can both lock it in.

Just them:

Perfect.

If they picked the home visit, say "at the visit" instead of "on the Zoom".

7
Book it

Later today, two clock times

Our filtration specialists have [time] or [time] free later today. We run late so couples can both jump on after work and the kids' activities. Just need those photos through beforehand.

They push for another day:

No worries, we can do that.
8
Close

Confirm and hang up

Perfect — I'll text you the Zoom link, the photos we need, and the info link again so [partner] can have a read before we jump on. Your filtration specialist will have a proposal ready for your home by [time].

If they ask

"How much is it?" or anything technical (filters, pressure, specs)
Great question, that's exactly what your specialist will go through on the Zoom.
"Will it be you on the Zoom?"
It'll be me, actually. I look after a lot of the Zooms myself.

Only if it's true. Never a fake name, never "I'm just admin".

They want the home visit
No worries. That's $200, and if you go ahead we take it off the price.

Book it in the In-Home calendar and send the $200 payment link.

After the call

  1. Book the Zoom in GHL as Confirmed. That sends the confirmation, the photo ask and every reminder automatically.
  2. Write the notes on their contact, in the same order as the present1 setup screen:
    1. Concern, main one first: skin / hair / better water / taste / smell / scale
    2. Their words, up to 3 short lines
    3. People at home
    4. Name and suburb (from the survey)
    5. Spend now: bottled $ per week, jug & shower filters $ per year
    6. Who's joining the Zoom
    7. What they liked from the info (or didn't read it)
    8. Photos in: yes / no
  3. No answer? Ring again straight away, then text them yourself:
    Hey [Name], it's [your name] from Neptune Filtration, just tried to give you a call about your water. When's a good time to ring you back?
  4. Before the Zoom: look at their photos (so "your proposal's ready" is true) and set up present1 from your notes.

Rules

Every call
  • Full confidence on every question. Never sound unsure.
  • You're the booker, not the expert: "our filtration specialists have…", never "I can do…".
  • Photos always come before price.
  • No price on this call. On the Zoom: $3,872 when they go ahead at their appointment (20% off). Decide later: $4,840, and hold it.
  • Never promise a skin or hair condition goes away.
  • Ring within about 3 minutes of the survey, any hour.
