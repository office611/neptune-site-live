After the call

  1. Book the Zoom in GHL as Confirmed. That sends the confirmation, the photo ask and every reminder automatically.
  2. Write the notes on their contact, in the same order as the present1 setup screen:
    1. Concern, main one first: skin / hair / better water / taste / smell / scale
    2. Their words, up to 3 short lines
    3. People at home
    4. Name and suburb (from the survey)
    5. Spend now: bottled $ per week, jug & shower filters $ per year
    6. Who's joining the Zoom
    7. What they liked from the info (or didn't read it)
    8. Photos in: yes / no
  3. No answer? Ring again straight away, then text them yourself:
    Hey [Name], it's [your name] from Neptune Filtration, just tried to give you a call about your water. When's a good time to ring you back?
  4. Before the Zoom: look at their photos (so "your proposal's ready" is true) and set up present1 from your notes.

Rules

Every call
  • Full confidence on every question. Never sound unsure.
  • You're the booker, not the expert: "our filtration specialists have…", never "I can do…".
  • Photos always come before price.
  • No price on this call. On the Zoom: $3,872 when they go ahead at their appointment (20% off). Decide later: $4,840, and hold it.
  • Never promise a skin or hair condition goes away.
  • Ring within about 3 minutes of the survey, any hour.
