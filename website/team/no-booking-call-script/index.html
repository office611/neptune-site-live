Neptune Filtration

No-booking call script — for leads who filled out the survey but didn't pick a time. Job of this call: book a Zoom with everyone involved and ask for photos. No price on this call.

1
Opening

Greeting + Why I'm Calling

Hey, is this [Name]? It's Stefan from Neptune Filtration — how you going? Saw you filled out our form about [what they ticked] but didn't get to lock in a time, so thought I'd give you a quick ring and sort it with you.

Plain and friendly. Don't make them feel caught out for not booking.
2
Story Arc

Let Them Self-Select

Quick one — have you already sat down with one of these filtration companies before, or is this your first time looking into it?

Yes → step 3. First time → straight to step 4.
3
If They've Sat With A Company

Follow-Up Questions

Which company was that, out of curiosity? What was it that put you off going ahead with them? And how long ago was that?

4
Dig Pain Point

What's Actually Going On

You mentioned [what they ticked] — what's been going on with that? How long's it been bothering you? Have you tried anything already?

You already know their reason from the survey — use it. One or two exchanges, not an interview.
5
Confidence, Then The Zoom, Then Photos

Why We Jump On A Zoom

Based on what you've told me, we can definitely help you get that sorted. All my customers jump on a quick Zoom with me and I run through everything with them — that's where I'll get you your exact price. Just need a couple of photos first so it's accurate, not a guess.

If they ask for a price now
That's exactly what the Zoom's for — I need to see your setup first so it's accurate, not a guess. It's quick.
6
Decision-Maker Check

Get Everyone On The Zoom

Is this a decision you're making with anyone else — partner, wife? Let's get them on the Zoom too so we're not doing this twice.

7
Book It — Same Day

Two Specific Times, Never "Whenever Works"

I can do [time] or [time] today — we run big hours every day for exactly this reason. Just need those photos through beforehand.

Real clock times ("4" or "7:30"), not "this afternoon".
If they push for days out
No worries, we can do that.
Book it, but treat it as lower intent.
8
Close This Call

Confirm And Get Off

Perfect — I'll send you a confirmation now with the Zoom link and the exact photos we need, and I'll have a proposal ready for your home by the time we jump on at [time].

→ Book it in the GHL calendar while you're still on the call, so the confirmation text goes out.
✉
If They Don't Answer

Text, Then Call Once More Later

Hey [Name], it's Stefan from Neptune Filtration. Saw you filled out the form but didn't get to pick a time — grab one here: neptunefiltration.com.au/melb/book

Hard Rule

No price, no discount talk, no guarantee pitch on this call — that's all for the Zoom. Every line delivered with full confidence.
