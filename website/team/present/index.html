What's inside

3 filters. 4 stages. Made properly.

STAGE 1
The Guard
Pleated polypropylene · 5 micron

Folded over and over to pack in as much surface area as possible. The first thing your water meets, catching the grit, rust and sand from the main so everything after it stays clean.

STAGES 2 + 3
The Disruptor
Carbon block with polyphosphate crystals · 1 micron

A dense carbon block infused with polyphosphate crystals that stop hard minerals turning into scale on your glass, taps and appliances, without taking the minerals out of your water.

STAGE 4
The Extractor
Coconut shell activated carbon · 1 micron

Coconut shells, charred at high heat, then activated with steam to open up millions of microscopic pores. One gram holds over 1,000 m² of surface, nearly four tennis courts. Not the coal-based carbon cheaper filters use.
