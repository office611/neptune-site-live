Frequently Asked Questions

Isn't Melbourne's water already safe and among the best in Australia?

Yes to both — Melbourne's treated water meets Australian drinking water standards and is some of the best-treated water in the country. But "safe" and "already filtered to what you'd want" aren't the same thing. Treatment can still leave chlorine, sediment and other contaminants that affect taste, smell and feel — refining that further is exactly what whole-home filtration does.

Why don't you come out and quote in person like everyone else?

You don't need a salesperson dropping a chlorine tablet in a cup of water to prove a point — that's something you could do yourself. We're not here to scare you about every way your water could hurt you, or offer a "today only" discount that'll mysteriously still be there tomorrow. The video above covers exactly what a technician would tell you in your home — none of the tactics designed to get you to sign there and then.

Who is whole-home filtration actually for?

Most people come to us because of dry skin, itchy skin, hair loss, eczema, a bad taste or smell in their water, wanting to protect their appliances, or just wanting peace of mind about what's coming through their taps.

Where does the system connect to my home?

Wherever you want it — typically at your water meter, but we'll fit it wherever works best for you.

Is there a drop in water pressure?

No — our 20" system is designed to maintain your home's normal water pressure at every tap.

Do you service all Melbourne suburbs?

Yes — right across Melbourne.

Is there any maintenance?

Filters do need changing over time, but how often depends on your home's setup and how many people are living there — we'll confirm exactly what that looks like for you on the call.

Can the system reduce chlorine?

Yes — whole home filtration is designed to address chlorine, chloramine and other common contaminants.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — every system we install comes with a warranty. The exact terms depend on your setup, and we'll go through the details with you on the call.

I've already had a quote or water test from another company — what's different?

Worth asking us how Neptune compares — mention it on the call and we'll walk you through exactly where we're different, without needing to book another in-home visit.

Can't I just use a filter jug or shower filter instead?

Jugs and shower filters only cover one outlet — but you're in contact with water so much more than that every day, at every tap, shower and appliance. Whole home filtration covers all of it, not just one spot.

I rent — can I still get in touch?

Our systems are designed for homeowners, since installation is fitted to the property's own plumbing — unless you have your landlord's permission, in which case get in touch and we'll take it from there.
