This policy explains how Neptune Filtration (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal information.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address and property address.
  • Information you share about your home, water and household, including photos you send us (for example of your water meter and property).
  • Records of our calls, video meetings and messages with you.
  • Payment details. Card and finance details are entered directly with our payment providers (Stripe, Zip and Afterpay). We never see or store your full card details.
  • Website usage information, collected through cookies and similar tools, such as the pages you visit and how you use our forms.

How we use it

  • To contact you, prepare your filtration set-up and price, and provide your quote.
  • To process payments, book and complete your installation, and provide warranty and maintenance support.
  • To keep accurate records, including recordings of calls and video meetings, for quality, training and record-keeping.
  • To send you relevant updates and offers. You can opt out at any time.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

Who we share it with

We only share your information where needed to provide our services, including with:

  • Licensed plumbers who carry out your installation.
  • Payment and finance providers (Stripe, Zip and Afterpay).
  • Software we use to run the business, such as booking, customer management, video meeting and accounting tools.
  • Advertising and analytics platforms (such as Meta and Microsoft Clarity), which help us understand how our website and ads perform.

We don’t sell your personal information. Some of these providers may store information outside Australia.

Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and only keep it for as long as we need it.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or raise a privacy concern, by contacting us on 0468 018 396 or office@neptunefiltration.com.au. We’ll respond within a reasonable time. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
