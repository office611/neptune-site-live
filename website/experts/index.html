Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I submit my details?

A member of our team will be in touch to discuss what's possible for your home.

Where does the system connect to my home?

Typically at your water meter, though the exact location can vary depending on your property — we're happy to install wherever works best for you.

Is there a drop in water pressure?

No — the system is sized to maintain your home's normal water pressure at every tap.

Will installation damage my garden?

Our team takes care around your property during installation.

How long does installation take?

Installation typically takes under two hours, carried out by licensed Melbourne plumbers.

Do you service all Melbourne suburbs?

We service homes across metropolitan Melbourne.

Can the system reduce chlorine?

Yes — whole home filtration is designed to address chlorine and other common water quality concerns.

Is the system designed for Melbourne's water?

Yes — built for Melbourne. We only install and support our systems here, nowhere else.

I've already had a quote from another company — what's different?

Worth asking us how Neptune compares — happy to explain the difference.

Can't I just use a filter jug or shower filter instead?

Jugs and shower filters only cover one outlet. Whole home filtration covers every tap in the house.

I rent — can I still get in touch?

Our systems are designed for homeowners, since installation is fitted to the property's own plumbing.
