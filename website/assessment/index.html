Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a "home assessment"?

It's a short in-home visit where one of our Melbourne water specialists tests your water, checks your plumbing setup and explains which filtration options, if any, are suitable for your property.

Is the home assessment free?

Yes. There's no cost for the assessment itself and no obligation to proceed afterwards.

Am I obligated to buy anything?

No. The assessment is there to inform you. Any decision about installation is entirely up to you, and there's no pressure to decide on the day.

What happens after I submit my details?

One of our Melbourne water specialists reviews your answers and contacts you to arrange a convenient time to visit your home.

Will you actually test my water?

Yes. Testing your water is part of every home assessment, so any recommendation is based on your property, not assumptions.

What does the specialist check during the visit?

Your water quality, your existing plumbing setup, and where a system could be fitted — then we explain what's suitable for your home.

How long does the appointment take?

A typical home assessment takes around 30 minutes, covering the water test, a plumbing review and a walk-through of suitable options.

Do you service all Melbourne suburbs?

We currently service homes across metropolitan Melbourne. Suburb suitability is confirmed as part of your assessment.

Is someone able to visit outside business hours?

Yes — we regularly schedule late-afternoon visits, since that's when most families are home from school and work. Weekend appointments may also be available; just mention it on the phone call.

How long does installation take?

We can usually have your system installed within a week of your assessment. On the day, installation typically takes up to three hours, carried out by one of our licensed Melbourne plumbers.

Will installation damage my garden?

Our specialists plan the installation around your property and take care to minimise disruption to existing landscaping.

Can I ask questions during the visit?

Of course. The home assessment is designed to be a conversation — ask as many questions as you'd like.

How often are filters replaced?

Filters are replaced annually as part of your scheduled servicing, so your system continues performing as it should.

Can the system reduce chlorine?

Whole home filtration is designed to address chlorine, along with sediment, heavy metals and other common water quality concerns.

Do I have to decide on the day?

No. There's no obligation to decide anything during the home assessment. You're welcome to take the time you need.

I've already had a quote from another company — is this different?

Often, yes. If you've already had your water tested elsewhere, we may not need to re-test it — but we'll still look at your plumbing setup in person. It's also a good chance to compare quotes directly and ask why a Neptune system might be the better fit for your home.

Can't I just use a filter jug or shower filter instead?

Jugs and shower filters only cover one outlet each and need frequent replacement. The home assessment shows you what's happening across the whole property before you decide what's worth addressing.

I rent — can I still complete the assessment?

Our whole home systems are designed for homeowners, since installation is fitted to the property's own plumbing. If you own your home, you're welcome to complete the assessment.

What if the assessment shows my home isn't suitable?

Then you'll know that too. Not every home needs the same system, and there's no obligation either way.
