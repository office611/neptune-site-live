The Difference Your Whole Family Will Feel

Skin & Hair

Softer skin and hair, with less dryness after every shower.

How Our Filters Help

Chlorine is a bleaching agent added to tap water for disinfection. Since skin is the body's largest organ, and hot water opens pores and increases absorption, showering in chlorinated water regularly can strip natural oils — leaving skin and hair drier than they'd otherwise be. Filtering it out before it reaches your shower removes that daily exposure.

Drinking Water

No chlorine taste or smell — drink straight from any tap.

How Our Filters Help

Chlorine and its by-products (like chloramine, formed when ammonia is added to mask the taste) are what create that noticeable taste and smell in tap water. Removing them at the source means every tap in the house tastes the same as filtered water — not just the ones you've specifically treated.

Appliances

Less mineral buildup, less cleaning — appliances last longer.

How Our Filters Help

Your filter's second stage uses a carbon block infused with polyphosphate crystals. As water passes through, the polyphosphate binds to the calcium and magnesium ions that cause limescale — reducing them crystallising onto glass, taps, and appliances. Nothing is removed or added to your water; the minerals stay exactly as they are, they just reduce binding into scale.

Neptune Filters — what do we filter out?

A system that suits Melbourne's water profile. We don't care about Perth water, Adelaide water, or anywhere else.

3 filters, 4-stage filtration, reducing up to 99% of chlorine and up to 85% of heavy metals.

Stage 1 — Pleated Sediment Filter (5 micron): made from pleated polypropylene, folded to maximise surface area inside the housing. Captures rust, dirt, sand, pipe scale and fine particulates entering from the supply main, protecting the carbon stages downstream from sediment loading.

Stage 2 — Scale Reduction Carbon Block (1 micron): a carbon block infused with polyphosphate crystals — as water passes through, the polyphosphate binds to calcium and magnesium ions, stopping them crystallising into limescale on glass, taps and appliances, without removing or adding minerals to your water. High-density block construction keeps pressure steady, and also reduces trace lead and mercury from ageing pipes.

Stage 3 — Coconut Shell Activated Carbon (1 micron): coconut shells are charred at high heat, then activated with steam to open up a microporous structure with surface area exceeding 1,000 m²/g — that activation process is what gives the carbon its adsorption power. Compressed into a dense block, it targets chlorine, chloramine, VOCs, trihalomethanes and broader heavy metals. Standard coal-based carbon, used in low-cost filters, has far less capacity to adsorb any of it.
