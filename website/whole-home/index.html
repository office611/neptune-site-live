See what’s inside: the 5 stages
  1. Sediment. Pleated polypropylene, 5 micron. Sand, rust and grit from the main.
  2. Limescale. Carbon block with polyphosphate crystals, 1 micron. Helps stop hard minerals forming scale.
  3. Chlorine. Dense carbon block. Reduces the chlorine added to treat the water.
  4. Chloramine. Activated coconut shell carbon, 1 micron. The longer-lasting treatment chemical.
  5. Heavy metals. Helps reduce metals picked up from older pipes.

Yes. $3,195 including GST, fully installed. A $639 deposit locks in your date, and you pay the $2,556 balance once it’s installed. If your photos show anything unusual, I’ll tell you before you pay anything.

Aren’t all whole home systems the same?

Not ours. 5 stages across 3 filters, including activated coconut shell carbon. Planned from your photos, so nobody comes to your home. Installed in about 5 days by our own plumbing team. Fresh filters delivered when it’s time, always our latest version. And Melbourne only.

Will it affect my water pressure?

No. It’s a 20-inch system sized for the whole home, so you keep full pressure at every tap and shower.

Melbourne water is safe to drink. Why filter it?

It is safe. Chlorine, and in parts of Melbourne chloramine, is added to keep it that way on its way to you, and it’s still in the water when it reaches your shower and your glass. Our system reduces it before it gets to your taps.

No. Answer a few quick questions, have a quick chat with me, and we plan everything from your photos. Our plumbers only come out on install day.
