So why doesn’t your home smell like a swimming pool?

In parts of Melbourne, ammonia is added to the water, creating chloramine (chlorine + ammonia) to mask the taste and smell.

Chlorine is a gas, so in a swimming pool it evaporates off the surface and they keep adding more to top it up. At an indoor pool, that gas hangs in the air, which is why you can smell it.

At home, you get fresh water every single time you turn on your tap. It isn’t sitting there being recycled and topped up like a pool.
