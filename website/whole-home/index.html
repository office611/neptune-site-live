The System

5 Stages. 3 Filters. Every Tap.

Installed on your mains, before the water reaches any tap, shower or appliance. Stainless steel cover, copper piping, bypass valve, and no power needed.

  1. 1
    SedimentPleated polypropylene, 5 micron. Sand, rust and grit from the main.
  2. 2
    LimescaleCarbon block with polyphosphate crystals, 1 micron. Helps stop hard minerals forming scale.
  3. 3
    ChlorineDense carbon block. Reduces the chlorine added to treat the water.
  4. 4
    ChloramineActivated coconut shell carbon, 1 micron. The longer-lasting treatment chemical.
  5. 5
    Heavy metalsHelps reduce metals picked up from older pipes.

Aren’t all whole home systems the same?

Not ours. 5 stages across 3 filters, including activated coconut shell carbon. Planned from your photos, so nobody comes to your home. Installed in about 5 days by our own plumbing team. Fresh filters delivered when it’s time, always our latest version. And Melbourne only, so we know the water here.

Why is it less than some quotes I’ve had?

No salesperson drives to your home, and nobody’s on commission, so that cost never gets added to your price. You still get a dedicated plumbing team, WaterMark certification and a 7-year workmanship warranty.

Will it affect my water pressure?

No. It’s a 20-inch system sized for the whole home, so you keep full pressure at every tap and shower.

Melbourne water is safe to drink. Why filter it?

It is safe. Chlorine, and in parts of Melbourne chloramine, is added to keep it that way on its way to you, and it’s still in the water when it reaches your shower and your glass. Our system reduces it before it gets to your taps.

Does someone need to come to my home?

No. Answer a few quick questions, have a quick chat with me, and we plan everything from your photos. Our plumbers only come out on install day.

How long does installation take?

2–3 hours on the day, done by our own dedicated plumbing team. On average it’s about 5 days from booking to installed.

How often do the filters need changing?

About once a year. A fresh set of all 3 is $300, delivered to your door, and you swap them yourself with our step-by-step video.

What if my home needs something different?

The price is for a standard install. If your photos show anything unusual, I’ll tell you before you pay anything.
