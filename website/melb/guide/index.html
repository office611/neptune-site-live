Why It Matters

Why Reducing It Makes A Huge Difference

Your skin soaks it up

Our test: two fingers in a cup of cold tap water, then the chlorine tablet. It stays clear. Your skin absorbed the chlorine, and that’s cold water. Hot showers open your pores even more, and you breathe in the steam.

Skin & hair

Chlorine is a bleach. It strips away the natural oils your skin and hair need, which can lead to dry skin, itchy skin, eczema flare-ups and hair loss.

Taste & smell

Chlorine is acidic. Reduce it, and your water tastes and smells better. Simple.

It’s in every glass your family drinks and every shower they take.
