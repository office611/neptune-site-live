5-Stage Filtration

3 Filters. 5 Stages. Every Tap.

The Guard

Pleated polypropylene · 5 micron
STAGE 1
SedimentSand, rust & grit

Folded over and over to pack in as much surface area as possible. The first thing your water meets, so everything after it stays clean.

The Disruptor

Carbon block with polyphosphate crystals · 1 micron
STAGE 2
LimescaleStops hard minerals forming scale
STAGE 3
ChlorineAdded to treat the water

Polyphosphate crystals stop scale on your glass, taps and hot water system, without taking the minerals out of your water.

The Extractor

Activated coconut shell carbon · 1 micron
STAGE 4
ChloramineThe longer-lasting treatment chemical
STAGE 5
Heavy metalsPicked up from older pipes

Coconut shells, charred at high heat, then activated with steam to open up millions of microscopic pores.
