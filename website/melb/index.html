FAQs

Common Questions

What does whole home filtration do?

It filters your water on your mains line, where it comes into your home. So every tap, shower and appliance runs on filtered water, not just the kitchen tap.

Melbourne water is safe to drink. Why filter it?

It is safe. Chlorine, and in parts of Melbourne chloramine, is added to keep it that way on its way to you, and it’s still in the water when it reaches your shower and your glass. Our system reduces it before it gets to your taps.

Will it affect my water pressure?

No. It’s sized for the whole home, so you keep full pressure at every tap and shower.

Does someone need to come to my home?

No. Answer a few quick questions, have a quick chat with our team, and get your price without anyone coming to your home. Our plumbers only come out on install day.

How long does installation take?

2–3 hours on the day, done by our own dedicated plumbing team. On average it’s about 5 days from going ahead to installed.

What about filter changes?

About once a year. A fresh set is delivered to your door, and you swap them yourself with our step-by-step video.

What if I’m not happy with it?

You’re covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee, plus a 7-year workmanship warranty on the install.
