What To Expect From The Free Test

Is the water test actually free?

Yes. No cost, no catch. A Neptune specialist comes to the home, tests the water, shows the results on the spot. That's it. Nothing to sign. Nothing to pay.

What exactly does the specialist test for?

Chlorine levels, hard mineral content (calcium and magnesium), sediment, and the disinfection byproducts most common in Melbourne's treated supply — the same things that affect skin, hair and taste every time you shower or run the tap.

Do I need to do anything to prepare?

No. Just be home for the 30 minutes. The specialist brings everything needed to test at the tap.

Is this different from a council water quality report?

Yes. Council reports show a treated-supply average across the whole network. This tests the actual water coming out of your actual taps — after it's travelled through your specific pipes, your hot water system, your fittings. It's often different from the average.

Will this actually help with my hair or skin, or is that just marketing?

Chlorine and hard minerals stripping natural oils from skin and coating hair is well documented — it's not unique to Neptune or Melbourne. The test simply shows whether it's happening in your home, and at what level, before anyone talks about a system.

Will there be any sales pressure?

No. The test exists to show what's in the water — not to sell a system. Once you see the results, the decision is entirely yours. Most families say the hardest part is deciding whether they actually want to know.

How long does it take?

About 30 minutes. The specialist tests at the tap, shows the numbers on the spot, and answers any questions. No waiting weeks for lab reports — the results are right there before the specialist leaves the home.

Does someone need to be home the whole time?

Yes, just the one visit, about 30 minutes, at a time that works for you.

What if the results show nothing unusual?

Then you'll know that too. Some homes test lower than expected — and knowing that is just as useful. Either way, you leave with the actual numbers for your home. Not a Melbourne average. Your water.

How soon would I notice a difference if I did go ahead?

Most families report noticing a change in skin and hair within one to two weeks of installation — it's usually the shower that people mention first.

Can't I just use a shower filter or a filter jug instead?

Jugs only cover drinking water — a small fraction of daily contact. Shower filters help but treat one outlet, need frequent cartridge changes, and don't touch the kitchen tap, laundry, or the rest of the home. The test shows you what's actually happening across the whole house before you decide what's worth fixing.

I rent — can I still get the test done?

The free test and installation are for homeowners, since any system needs to be fitted to the home's own plumbing. If you own your home, you're eligible.

I've already had a water test or quote from another company — is this different?

Often, yes. A lot of Melbourne homeowners comparing quotes find the numbers and the sales approach vary more than expected between companies. Worth seeing Neptune's results and process directly before deciding anything.
