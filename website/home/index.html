Frequently Asked Questions

Isn't Melbourne's water already safe and among the best in Australia?

Yes to both — Melbourne's treated water meets Australian drinking water standards and is some of the best-treated water in the country. But "safe" and "already filtered to what you'd want" aren't the same thing. Treatment can still leave chlorine, sediment and other contaminants that affect taste, smell and feel — refining that further is exactly what whole-home filtration does.

Who is whole-home filtration actually for?

Most people come to us because of dry skin, itchy skin, hair loss, eczema, a bad taste or smell in their water, wanting to protect their appliances, or just wanting peace of mind about what's coming through their taps. If any of that sounds familiar, it's worth a free water investigation.

Why is whole-home filtration becoming such a big thing?

More homeowners are realising a jug or under-sink filter only treats one tap — everything else in the house still runs on unfiltered water. Whole-home systems treat it all at the source, which is why more Melbourne homes are switching over.

Where does the system connect to my home?

Wherever you want it — typically at your water meter, but we'll fit it wherever works best for you.

Is there a drop in water pressure?

No — our 20" system is designed to maintain your home's normal water pressure at every tap.

Will installation damage my garden?

Nothing gets damaged. Our team has handled every type of property and install — there's nothing about your setup that would be new to us.

Do you service all Melbourne suburbs?

Yes — right across Melbourne, no dramas.

Is there any maintenance?

Filters do need changing over time, but how often depends on what your water investigation finds, your home's setup, and how many people are living there — we'll confirm exactly what that looks like for you.

Can the system reduce chlorine?

Yes — whole home filtration is designed to address chlorine, chloramine and other common contaminants.

What's actually found in Melbourne's water supply?

Chlorine and sediment are present in any Melbourne home connected to mains water — that's standard as part of treatment and distribution. Beyond that, it varies home to home depending on your pipes and suburb, which is exactly what your free water investigation is for.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes — every system we install comes with a warranty. The exact terms depend on your setup, and we'll go through the details with you as part of your free water investigation.

I've already had a quote or water test from another company — what's different?

Worth asking us how Neptune compares. Either way, we still need to come out and see your home in person to confirm the best system for your setup and answer any questions you have.

Can't I just use a filter jug or shower filter instead?

Jugs and shower filters only cover one outlet — but you're in contact with water so much more than that every day, at every tap, shower and appliance. Whole home filtration covers all of it, not just one spot.

I rent — can I still get in touch?

Our systems are designed for homeowners, since installation is fitted to the property's own plumbing — unless you have your landlord's permission, in which case get in touch and we'll take it from there.

Why does Neptune stand out from other companies?

We're not a plumber selling filters as a side offering, and we're not a big national company treating Melbourne as one market among many. Melbourne is the only place we work, because we understand what your home actually needs — with our own dedicated plumbing crew doing every job. Every state's water is different, so we'd rather be specialists here than generalists everywhere.
