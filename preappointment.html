Your Water

What's actually in your Melbourne water.

Melbourne's water is treated and meets safety standards — but treated water isn't the same as optimised water. Most homes have things in their supply that affect taste, skin, and how appliances perform over time.

🪨

Sediment

As water moves through older pipes, it can carry fine particles. Over time these accumulate inside appliances, hot water systems, and plumbing — gradually affecting performance.

⚠️

Trace Metals

In areas with older street infrastructure, trace amounts of heavy metals can enter the supply before reaching your property. Levels vary depending on your suburb and plumbing age.

💧

Hard Minerals

Calcium and magnesium occur naturally and are common in Melbourne's supply. They leave scale on tapware, shower screens, and inside appliances over time.

🧪

Chlorine

Used to disinfect the supply. It works — but chlorine is also what causes that faint pool smell in the shower, affects drinking water taste, and can dry out skin and hair with daily exposure.

⚗️

Disinfection By-Products

Chlorine can react with organic matter in the supply to form low-level compounds. Whole-home filtration addresses these before water reaches any tap.

The Full Picture

Why most homes aren't fully protected.

Most filtering solutions address drinking water at one tap. But the reality is — your family's exposure goes much further than that.

🚰

Tap Filters & Jugs

Bench-top filters and filter jugs only treat water at a single point. Your showers, baths, laundry, and dishwasher all still run on completely unfiltered water.

⚠ Partial coverage only
🪣

Basic Inline Filters

Single-stage filters are a step up, but they typically only address one contaminant type — often sediment — leaving chlorine and mineral issues untouched throughout the home.

⚠ Single-stage limitation
🏠

Whole Home Filtration

A mains-connected whole home system treats all water before it reaches any outlet — kitchen, shower, bath, laundry, hot water system. Complete coverage in one solution.
