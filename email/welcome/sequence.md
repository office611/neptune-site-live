# Email Flow — Welcome / Lead Nurture Sequence

## Trigger
Contact books a free water test OR submits any lead form.

## Flow Overview

```
Day 0  → Email 1: Confirmation + What to expect
Day 1  → Email 2: Education — "What's actually in your water"
Day 3  → Email 3: Social proof — customer story
Day 5  → Email 4: Objection handler — cost & commitment
Day 7  → Email 5: Last call / urgency
```

## Email 2 — Education (Day 1)

**Subject:** What we're actually looking for in your water
**Preview text:** Most of it is invisible. That's the problem.

---

Hi [First Name],

Tomorrow we're coming to test your water. I wanted to give you a quick heads up on what we look for — and why it matters.

**The 5 most common issues we find in [City] homes:**

**1. Chlorine & chloramine**
Added at the treatment plant. High levels affect taste, smell, and for some people, skin sensitivity. Most [City] tap water has [X–Xppm] — the limit is [X].

**2. Hard water (calcium & magnesium)**
Scale buildup on taps and appliances is the visible sign. Invisible: reduced appliance lifespan, dry skin and hair, higher energy bills from your water heater working harder.

**3. Lead**
Old pipes can leach lead into your water even if the source is clean. There's no safe level of lead exposure for children.

**4. PFAS (forever chemicals)**
Increasingly detected in water supplies across [country]. Linked to long-term health concerns. Most water tests from the council don't include PFAS.

**5. Bacteria & sediment**
More common in older homes or properties on tank/bore water.

We'll test for all of these tomorrow. You'll know exactly where you stand.

See you [day],
[Name]
