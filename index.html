Your Water

What's actually flowing through your home right now.

Melbourne's water is treated and safe by regulation — but treatment isn't the same as clean. Here's what's typically present in the water reaching your family every day.

🧪

Chlorine & Disinfectants

Added deliberately to kill bacteria. It works — but the chlorine that arrives at your tap dries out skin, creates that pool-like smell in your shower, and affects the taste of your drinking water and cooking.

🪨

Sediment & Pipe Particles

As water travels through aging infrastructure, it picks up rust, silt and fine particles. These gradually accumulate inside your appliances, hot water system, and plumbing fittings.

⚠️

Heavy Metals (Lead & Mercury)

Heavy metals can enter your water through older street infrastructure and supply pipes before reaching your home. This can include trace amounts of lead and mercury, which are classified as Group 1 carcinogens and may pose long-term health risks with consistent exposure.

💧

Hard Minerals

Calcium and magnesium are the cause of that white scale on your kettle, shower screen and tapware. Over time, they reduce appliance efficiency and leave surfaces harder to clean.

⚗️

Disinfection By-Products

When chlorine reacts with organic matter in the water supply, it can form compounds like trihalomethanes. These are present at low levels, but whole-home filtration removes them before they reach your taps.

The Full Picture

Why most homes aren't fully protected.

Most filtering solutions address drinking water at one tap. But the reality is — your family's exposure goes much further than that.

🚰

Tap Filters & Jugs

Bench-top filters and filter jugs only treat water at a single point. Your showers, baths, laundry, and dishwasher all still run on completely unfiltered water.

⚠ Partial coverage only
🪣

Basic Inline Filters

Single-stage filters are a step up, but they typically only address one contaminant type — often sediment — leaving chlorine and mineral issues untouched throughout the home.

⚠ Single-stage limitation
🏠

Whole Home Filtration

A mains-connected whole home system treats all water before it reaches any outlet — kitchen, shower, bath, laundry, hot water system. Complete coverage in one solution.

✓ Complete coverage
Often overlooked

The shower is where most exposure happens.

Most people focus on drinking water — but your skin and lungs absorb far more during a 10-minute shower than from drinking a glass of water. Chlorine vapour, absorbed through skin and inhaled, is a daily reality for most households.
