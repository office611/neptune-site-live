# NEPTUNE FILTRATION
## In-Home Consultation Script
### For Consultant Use — Version 1.0

---

## HOW TO USE THIS SCRIPT

This is your consultation framework — not a word-for-word performance piece. Read every section until you know it naturally. The goal is to sound like a knowledgeable friend who happens to be a licensed water specialist, not someone reading off a clipboard.

**Sections marked [ANCHOR]** — critical psychological moments. Do not skip or rush these.
**Sections marked [FLEXIBLE]** — adapt language naturally to how the conversation flows.
**Language in "quotes"** — suggested phrasing proven to land well.
**[Notes in brackets]** — consultant guidance, not spoken aloud.

**Timing guide:** The full consultation runs 40–55 minutes. Enough to be thorough, not enough to lose their attention.

## STAGE 1: ARRIVAL & OPENING RAPPORT
### Time: 0–5 minutes

**THE FIRST 30 SECONDS SETS THE TONE FOR THE WHOLE CONSULTATION.**

When the door opens — smile, be warm, be professional. You're a water specialist making a house call, not a salesperson dropping in.

**Script — Opening:**

> "Hey [Name], how are you? I'm [Your Name] from Neptune Filtration — good to finally meet you."

[Pause. Let them invite you in. Don't push past them.]

> "Thanks for having me in. I'll try not to take up too much of your time. What we'll do is run through the water test first, I'll explain what the results actually mean, and then I can run you through our systems and pricing if you want to go that far. Does that work?"

[ANCHOR] You've just set the agenda — test, then education, then options. This is deliberate. It frames you as an educator first. The homeowner now knows what to expect and their guard comes down.

> "Is anywhere in particular easiest to set up — kitchen or laundry, wherever the main tap is handy?"

[Move toward where they indicate. As you walk through the home, observe naturally — visible scale on taps, kettle condition, shower glass, kids' toys, pets. These are anchors you'll return to later without making it obvious you clocked them.]

**If they seem cautious or guarded:**

> "I know you probably get a lot of people coming through wanting to sell you something. This isn't that kind of visit — I'm just here to test your water and show you what's actually in it. Whether you do anything with that information is completely up to you."

## STAGE 2: DISCOVERY — UNDERSTANDING THEIR SITUATION
### Time: 5–10 minutes

[ANCHOR] Before you touch the water test kit, spend 3–5 minutes genuinely understanding this homeowner's situation. Ask, listen, connect. Everything you learn here shapes every part of the presentation that follows.

**Opening discovery question:**

> "Before I get started, I always like to ask — what was the main thing that made you want to get the water looked at? Was there something specific, or more of a general 'let's just see what we're dealing with'?"

[FLEXIBLE — let them talk. The more they share, the better you can tailor what follows.]

---

**"Kids / new baby in the house"**
> "That's a really common trigger, and it makes complete sense. When you've got little ones, you naturally start thinking differently about what they're drinking and bathing in. We'll go through everything."

**"Dry skin, itchy skin, hair issues"**
> "Interesting — that comes up more than people would expect, and there's actually a direct connection to chlorine levels. We'll look at that specifically."

**"Just moved in"**
> "Perfect time to get it done, actually. You've got no idea what the previous owners dealt with or what the pipes are like in this area. Fresh start — let's see exactly what you've got."

**"Appliance damage / scale buildup"**
> "Yeah — and that's a real cost that doesn't get talked about enough. The mineral load in Melbourne water takes a toll on dishwashers, washing machines, hot water systems. We'll look at the hardness reading today."

**"Friend/neighbour recommended it"**
> "Good referral to get. We're doing a lot of installs in [suburb] at the moment. Your neighbour's water profile would be almost identical to yours."

> "How long have you been here?"

> "Day-to-day — tap water for drinking, or mostly bottled?"

[CONSULTANT NOTE] The bottled water question is important. If they're buying bottled water, they already know something isn't right with their tap water — they've just been managing it in the most expensive and inconvenient way possible. This anchors the value conversation perfectly when you get to pricing.]

> "Have you ever had someone come out and test your water before, or is this the first time?"

[If they say yes:
> "Oh interesting — what did that test show?" / "And did they explain what that actually meant for your home?"
This positions Neptune's educational approach as more thorough and consultative than whatever they experienced before.]

## STAGE 3: TRANSITION TO THE WATER TEST
### Time: 10–12 minutes

[This transition moves from conversation into demonstration. It should feel natural and clinical — like a doctor moving from intake questions to the examination.]

> "Alright — let me show you what we're actually dealing with. I'll run a quick test on your mains water. Takes a couple of minutes and it gives us something concrete to work from rather than me just telling you what Melbourne water is generally like."

[Begin setting up your test kit on the bench or near the sink. Work confidently and methodically — you've done this dozens of times.]

> "What we're going to look at is your chlorine level, your total dissolved solids — which is essentially everything dissolved in your water that isn't H₂O — and I'll show you a couple of other things as we go."

[CONSULTANT NOTE] While you're setting up, keep talking naturally. Fill the silence with something light and relevant. Ask something easy:

> "Do you notice the water tastes different in different parts of Melbourne?" / "Have you ever left a glass of tap water out overnight and noticed the smell it gives off?"

[These questions are conversational, but they also plant seeds. They prime the homeowner to start noticing things they've been ignoring.]

## STAGE 4: CONDUCTING THE WATER TEST
### Time: 12–27 minutes

[ANCHOR] This is the most important section of the entire consultation. The water test is not just diagnostic — it's the emotional and educational centrepiece of the visit. Take your time. Let results land before moving on. Do not rush past them.

**Run the tap for 30–60 seconds before testing.**

> "We run it for a bit first — clears out the water that's been sitting in the pipes so we're testing what's actually coming through live from the mains."

---

### Chlorine Test

[Hold up the test strip before dipping it.]

> "So this first one is the chlorine test. Melbourne Water treats the supply with chlorine — that's completely standard practice and it serves a purpose. It keeps the supply clean from the treatment plant all the way to your street. But by the time it reaches your tap, your home isn't part of the distribution system anymore. You don't need it. And the concentration is typically higher than most people expect."

[Dip the strip. Let it develop. Don't comment yet. Let the moment breathe.]

> "The Australian standard allows up to 5 milligrams per litre. What we typically find in Melbourne suburban homes is..."

[Read the result calmly. Not dramatically alarmed — matter-of-fact, like a doctor reading a test result.]

**If elevated (common in Melbourne):**

> "Yeah — so you're sitting at around [X] mg/L. That's within the legal limit, and I always want to be clear about that — Melbourne Water is doing their job, this water is technically safe to drink. But 'safe' and 'optimal' are two different things. That level of chlorine is exactly what gives your water that faint pool-like smell and that slight edge in the taste. And it's what's behind the skin and hair effects in the shower."

[Pause. Let it land.]

> "Here's something most people don't realise — and this is a published finding, not a scare tactic. You absorb roughly 6 times more chlorine through your skin in a 10-minute shower than you would from drinking a glass of tap water. The heat opens your pores, the steam carries it in. So if you've noticed dry skin, itchy scalp, or your hair just doesn't feel right even with a good product — there's a very reasonable chance that's exactly what's causing it."

[PAUSE. Let them connect this to their own experience. Don't jump to the next test immediately.]

**If kids are in the home — add this:**

> "For little ones especially — their skin is much more permeable than ours. Bath time is actually one of the highest chlorine exposure points for kids. It's not cause for alarm — but when it's easy to remove, it's worth removing."

---

### TDS / Mineral Test (Total Dissolved Solids)

> "This one is the TDS meter — total dissolved solids. Think of it as a measure of everything dissolved in your water that isn't H₂O. Minerals, sediment, trace metals, treatment residuals. Ideally under around 300 parts per million."

[Take reading.]

> "So you're at [X] ppm. In practical terms — that's the calcium and magnesium that builds scale on your shower glass, your tap fittings, inside your kettle. It's also what's working against your dishwasher and hot water system. A lot of people replace appliances without realising the hard water was the reason they failed early. It's one of those invisible costs."

[If they've mentioned scale or appliances already:]
> "Which is exactly what you mentioned earlier with [the shower / the kettle / the dishwasher] — that's a direct result of this reading."

---

### Sediment / Clarity Assessment

> "This last one is more visual — it just shows you the physical particulate. A lot of it you can't see with the naked eye, but it's there. In older suburb pipe systems, there's quite a bit of fine rust and sediment making it through. You'd notice it sometimes as a very slight cloudiness, or the water not looking completely clear in a white glass."

[Show the vial or visual demonstration. Keep it matter-of-fact.]

---

### Summary — Bringing It Together

[ANCHOR] The tests are done. Now translate the results into impact. This is the emotional pivot point of the consultation.

> "So let's put that together. What you've got is water that's technically safe — Melbourne Water does their job, and that's a real thing worth acknowledging. But what you also have is elevated chlorine that's affecting the taste, the smell, and what you're absorbing every time you shower. You've got mineral load building scale on your appliances. And you've got fine sediment in everything — your coffee, your cooking, your kids' water, your ice."

[Brief pause.]

> "The question isn't really 'is this dangerous?' — it's 'do you want to keep dealing with this when there's a simple, permanent fix?' And that's what I want to show you."

## STAGE 5: PRODUCT PRESENTATION — THE NEPTUNE SYSTEM
### Time: 27–42 minutes

[CONSULTANT NOTE] Transition into the product naturally — not as a sales pitch, but as the logical next step after the education. The problem is now established. Now you're presenting the solution.

[If you have a housing sample, bring it out now. Physical objects shift the energy and make it real.]

> "The system is a three-stage setup. Each stage does something specific. Let me walk you through them."

---

### Stage 1 — 5 Micron Pleated Sediment

> "First stage — sediment filtration at 5 micron. This catches the physical stuff. Rust particles, fine grit, anything making it through from the mains pipes. Your water comes in, the particles get trapped in the pleated media, and what comes out is physically clean water going into stages two and three."

---

### Stage 2 — 1 Micron Scale Reduction Carbon Block

> "Second stage — scale reduction carbon block at 1 micron. This is what addresses the mineral hardness we saw in your TDS reading. The calcium and magnesium — the stuff that builds scale on your appliances and your shower glass — is significantly reduced here. This is the stage protecting your dishwasher, your washing machine, your hot water system. People often don't connect early appliance failure to hard water, but it's one of the most common and most preventable causes."

---

### Stage 3 — 1 Micron Coconut Carbon Block

> "Third stage — coconut carbon block, also at 1 micron. This one does the most work on water quality. It removes chlorine — not reduces, removes. It captures VOCs, disinfection by-products — the compounds that form when chlorine reacts with organic matter in the supply. Herbicides, pesticides, trace heavy metals including lead and mercury. All at 1 micron filtration."

> "The reason we specifically use coconut carbon — not standard activated charcoal or granular carbon — is that it has significantly higher surface area and better adsorption efficiency. It's a more expensive media, but it does what it's supposed to do. That's the difference between systems that work and systems that look like they work."

We don't compete on being the cheapest. We compete on doing it properly."

[CONSULTANT NOTE] Confident, not arrogant. It respects their intelligence and closes the door on price comparison by anchoring firmly on quality and accountability. Say it plainly and let it stand.

## QUICK REFERENCE — CONSULTANT REMINDERS

### Throughout the visit:

- **Use their name** naturally — not constantly, but regularly.
- **Reference what they told you** — "You mentioned the kids' bath earlier..." / "You said you'd noticed that..." It shows you listened. It makes the consultation personal.
- **Slow down on the water test results** — don't rush past the key emotional moments. Let the numbers land.
- **Stay in questions** — "What do you think?" / "Have you noticed that yourself?" / "Does that make sense?" — keeps them engaged and talking.
- **Silence is powerful** — after a key statement, don't fill it. Let it sit.
- **Never mention competitors by name.**
- **Never volunteer competitor comparisons unprompted** — but be ready with them when it comes up, and lead it as the Strategic Transparency section.

### Tone always:

- Confident, not arrogant
- Educational, not lecturing
- Transparent, not defensive
- Warm, not fake
- Calm, not pushy

### What NOT to do:
