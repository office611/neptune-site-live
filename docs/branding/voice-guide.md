# Neptune Filtration — Brand Voice Guide

## The One Rule

Write like you're explaining water quality to a trusted friend.
Not a stranger. Not a customer. A friend.

---

## Before You Write — Ask These

1. Would a smart 12-year-old understand this?
2. Does every sentence earn its place?
3. Is the CTA impossible to misunderstand?
4. Would this make someone more likely to trust us?

If the answer to any of these is no, rewrite.

---

## Sentence Principles

**Short sentences convert.**
Don't write: "Neptune Filtration offers a comprehensive range of water purification solutions."
Write: "We filter your water. Every tap. Every time."

**Lead with benefit, not feature.**
Don't write: "Our 7-stage filtration system uses activated carbon."
Write: "You get cleaner, better-tasting water — from every tap in your home."

**Specific beats general.**
Don't write: "Many families in the area trust us."
Write: "[X,000] families in [City] use Neptune."

**Active voice always.**
Don't write: "Your water will be tested by our technician."
Write: "Our tech tests your water on the spot."

---

## Headline Formulas

These formulas work. Use them, then vary them.

**[Outcome] + [Timeframe]**
"Clean water in your home — installed today."

**[Problem] → [Solution]**
"Hard water destroying your appliances? Fixed in hours."

**Proof number + claim**
"[X,000] [City] families have already made the switch."

**Question + Curiosity**
"What's actually in your tap water?"

**"How to" + Benefit**
"How to get clean water from every tap — for under $[X]/month."

**Guarantee statement**
"Your water will be clean. Or we come back. Free."

---

## CTA Writing Rules

Every CTA should:
1. Start with a verb (Book, Get, Find, See, Try)
2. Reinforce zero risk ("free", "no obligation", "no commitment")
3. Be specific about what happens next ("Book Your Free Test" not just "Click Here")

**Good CTAs:**
- "Book Your Free Water Test"
- "Get a Free Quote"
- "See What's In Your Water"
- "Find Out in 30 Minutes"

**Bad CTAs:**
- "Submit"
- "Click Here"
- "Learn More" (acceptable as secondary but never primary)
- "Contact Us" (too vague — what happens when I contact you?)

---

## Numbers & Proof

Always use numerals, not words: "3 steps" not "three steps", "99%" not "ninety-nine percent".

Specificity builds trust:
- "$4,200 saved vs. bottled water" beats "saves money"
- "installed in 3 hours" beats "fast installation"
- "[X,000] local families" beats "thousands of customers"

Never invent numbers. Every stat must be verifiable.

---

## Guarantee Language

When referencing guarantees, use definitive language:

**Do:** "If it doesn't work, we fix it. Free."
**Don't:** "We'll try our best to resolve any issues you may encounter."

**Do:** "90-day satisfaction guarantee — full refund, no questions."
**Don't:** "We offer a satisfaction guarantee on our products."

---

## Punctuation & Formatting

- Em dashes (—) for emphasis: "One test — that's all it takes."
- Lists: Use when 3+ items. Max 7 items before breaking into sections.
- Ellipses (…): Use sparingly for pause/tension. Not for trailing off.
- ALL CAPS: Never. Use bold for emphasis instead.
- Exclamation marks: Maximum one per piece of copy. Reserve for genuine excitement.

---

## Words to Use / Avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| Clean, pure, safe | Hygienic, sanitised |
| Fix, solve | Address, remediate |
| Family, kids, home | Residential, occupants |
| Trusted, proven | Industry-leading |
| Fast, same-day | Expedited |
| Free | Complimentary |
| Guarantee | Assurance, warranty (use for legal docs only) |
| Find out, discover | Leverage, utilise |

---

## Tone by Situation

**High urgency (contamination/health concern):**
Calm but direct. Never panic. "Here's what we found. Here's what it means. Here's the fix."

**Sales/promotional:**
Confident and specific. Lead with the offer. Follow with proof. Close with CTA.

**Educational content:**
Helpful, thorough, non-judgmental. We're the expert friend, not the lecturer.

**Review responses:**
Warm, specific, grateful. Mention their name and the service. Invite them back.

**Complaint handling:**
Accountable, fast, solution-focused. Never defensive. Never blame.
