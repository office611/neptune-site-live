# Neptune Filtration — Brand Foundation

## Positioning Statement

For Melbourne homeowners who are concerned about what's in their tap water,
Neptune Filtration installs a single whole-home filtration system that cleans
every tap and shower in the home — backed by a 7-year workmanship warranty
and the WaterMark certification (AS 3497). Unlike point-of-use filters or jugs,
Neptune works at the mains — so the whole home is covered from day one.

---

## Brand Values

1. **Health first** — we exist to protect families, not sell hardware
2. **Transparency** — no surprises, no hard sells, no hidden costs
3. **Professionalism** — licensed plumbers, Australian-certified systems, done properly

## Voice & Tone

### The One-Line Rule
Write like a knowledgeable friend who happens to be a licensed plumber.
Not a salesperson. Not a scientist. A professional friend.

### Voice Attributes

| Attribute | We Are | We Are Not |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Confident | "Your water will be cleaner from day one." | "We think this might help..." |
| Specific | "Removes chlorine, sediment, and trace metals." | "Advanced multi-stage technology" |
| Warm | "Your family deserves to know what they're drinking." | "Residential filtration solutions" |
| Direct | "Book your free water test." | "Feel free to reach out at your convenience." |
| Calm | "Here's what we found. Here's what it means." | "Your water is DANGEROUS." |
| Professional | "WaterMark certified. VIC Licensed & Insured." | "Best filters in Melbourne!" |

### Tone Rules by Funnel Stage

| Stage | Tone |
|-------|------|
| Awareness (ads, social) | Curious, direct, benefit-first |
| Consideration (homepage) | Trustworthy, educational, calm |
| Pre-appointment page | Warm, reassuring, preparatory |
| In-appointment | Transparent, professional, no-pressure |
| Post-install (email/SMS) | Helpful, friendly, celebratory |
| Filter reorder | Frictionless, reminder-friendly, brief |

### Tone Shifts by Channel

- **Google Ads:** Intent-matching, specific, action-oriented
- **Meta Ads:** Curiosity-led, problem-aware, story-driven
- **Email:** Conversational, helpful, educational
- **SMS:** Ultra-brief, single action, never pushy
- **Website:** Trust-building, clear, process-transparent
- **Pre-appointment page:** Warm, informative, objection-reducing

## Trust Signals — Early Stage Rules

Neptune is building its review base. Trust is built through:

**DO use:**
- WaterMark Certified (AS 3497) badge
- VIC Licensed & Insured
- 7-Year Workmanship Warranty
- Transparent process explanation (step by step)
- Before/after imagery (real installs)
- Suburb familiarity (Melbourne suburbs served)
- Real customer names + suburbs in testimonials (only genuine ones)
- Founder/team positioning
- Education-based authority content
- Clear, specific product claims (no vague promises)
- Australian Standard reference (AS 3497)

**DO NOT:**
- Fabricate review counts ("200+ happy customers")
- Invent testimonials
- Use stock photo reviews
- Inflate statistics
- Use countdown timers that reset
