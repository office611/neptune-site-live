# Reusable Ad Frameworks

## 5 Core Formulas (Work Across All Channels)

---

### Formula 1: PAS (Problem → Agitation → Solution)
Best for: Cold audiences, Meta, YouTube

```
PROBLEM:   "[City] tap water contains [X] — most families don't know."
AGITATION: "Left untreated, it [consequence: damages appliances / affects health / costs more]."
SOLUTION:  "Neptune fixes it in [timeframe]. Free test. Guaranteed results."
```

---

### Formula 2: BAB (Before → After → Bridge)
Best for: Social proof ads, testimonial hooks

```
BEFORE: "Our water smelled like a swimming pool. We bought cases of bottled water every week."
AFTER:  "Now every tap tastes clean. We haven't bought a bottle in [X] months."
BRIDGE: "Neptune Filtration — free test, installed in hours."
```

---

### Formula 3: AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)
Best for: Long-form copy, email, landing pages

```
ATTENTION: Bold claim or provocative question
           "What's actually in your [City] tap water?"

INTEREST:  Specific, credible detail
           "Our tests found chlorine at 3x recommended levels in [X]% of [City] homes."

DESIRE:    Outcome + proof + guarantee
           "[X,000] families switched. Cleaner water, lower bills, lifetime service included."

ACTION:    One clear CTA
           "Book your free test this week →"
```

---

### Formula 4: Direct Offer (Highest intent audiences)
Best for: Retargeting, Google search, SMS

```
[Specific offer] + [Proof] + [Guarantee] + [CTA]

Example:
"Free in-home water test — [X,000] local homes served — zero obligation.
Book yours this week: [link]"
```

---

### Formula 5: Story → Lesson → CTA
Best for: Reels, video ads, educational content

```
STORY:  Short, specific customer anecdote (30–60 words)
LESSON: What this reveals about the broader problem
CTA:    How they can avoid the same issue / get the solution
```

## Headline Swipe File

**Urgency:**
- "[X] free water test slots left this week"
- "Price increase on [date] — lock in now"
- "Last [X] spots for same-week installation"

**Specificity:**
- "Removes 99.9% of chlorine, lead, and PFAS"
- "Installed in [X] hours or it's free"
- "[X,000] [City] families. [X] years. Zero complaints."

**Curiosity:**
- "What we found in [City]'s water last week"
- "The real reason your appliances fail early"
- "Why [X]% of homeowners don't know their water quality"

**Pain:**
- "Hard water is silently costing you thousands"
- "That chlorine smell? It's not normal."
- "Bottled water is not the answer. Here's why."

**Proof:**
- "★★★★★ — [X] reviews in [City]"
- "Trusted by [X,000] local families"
- "Recommended by [X] local plumbers"

## Offer Line Templates

Mix and match:

- "Free [in-home / on-site] water test — zero cost, zero obligation"
- "Free installation (save $[X]) — this month only"
- "No interest finance — $[X]/month, nothing to pay today"
- "Book today, installed [this week / within 48 hours]"
- "90-day money-back guarantee — no questions asked"
- "Lifetime service included — we fix any issue, forever"

## Platform-Specific Length Guide

| Platform | Headline | Body | CTA |

| Google Search | 30 chars | 90 chars | 15 chars |

| Meta Reels | Text overlay only | 3–5 words per frame | End card |
| Email | 41 chars (mobile) | No limit | 1 button |
| SMS | — | 160 chars total | URL in body |
| GBP Post | — | 300 chars visible | Button |
