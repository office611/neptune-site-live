# Meta Ads — Facebook & Instagram Campaign Copy

## Campaign Structure

```
Campaign 1: Lead Generation — Free Water Test (primary)
  Ad Set A: Homeowners 35–55, [City + radius], interests: home improvement, health
  Ad Set B: Lookalike — past customers (1%)
  Ad Set C: Retargeting — website visitors (30 days)

Campaign 2: Awareness — Water Quality Education
  Ad Set A: Broad, 28–60, homeowner-likely, [City]
  (Seed brand recall, warm traffic for retargeting)

Campaign 3: Retargeting — Landing page visitors who didn't book
  Ad Set A: Visited free-test page, no conversion (7 days)
  Ad Set B: Visited any page, no conversion (14 days)
```

---

## Ad Formats & When to Use

| Format | Use Case |
|--------|----------|
| Single image | Direct response, offer-led, retargeting |
| Carousel | Product comparison, before/after, step-by-step |
| Video (15–30s) | Awareness, testimonials, problem-agitation |
| Reels (vertical) | Educational hooks, behind-the-scenes |
| Lead form | When you want leads in-platform (lower friction) |

## Hooks Bank (For Testing)

Use these as opening lines to rotate and test:

Problem hooks:
- "Your tap water has [X] contaminants. Do you know which ones?"
- "Hard water is costing [City] homeowners thousands. Here's why."
- "If your water smells like a pool, you need to see this."
- "The reason your skin is dry might be your water, not your skin."

Curiosity hooks:
- "We tested water in [X] [City] homes. Here's what we found."
- "What happens when you stop buying bottled water (and do this instead)?"
- "I tested my neighbour's water. The results were alarming."

Offer hooks:
- "Free water test for [City] homeowners — [X] slots left this week."
- "[X,000] local families got this done. It takes 30 minutes."
- "One appointment changed the way our family drinks water."

---

## Creative Direction

**Do:** Real homes, real families, real technicians, water in motion
**Do:** Before/after split screens, test kit results shown clearly
**Do:** Text overlays on Reels (assume no sound)
**Don't:** Generic stock photos, overly staged scenes
**Don't:** Fear-mongering language (use concern-raising, not panic)

Mobile-first: Design for 9:16. Crop for 1:1 and 4:5 second.
