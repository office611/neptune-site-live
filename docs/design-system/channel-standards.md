# Neptune Filtration — Channel Consistency Standards

**Status:** Documentation only. No live changes.
**Purpose:** How the Neptune design system adapts to each marketing channel,
so every touchpoint feels like the same premium brand.

---

## The Channel Consistency Rule

Every channel has different constraints (character limits, dimensions, formats).
What stays constant across all of them:

1. **Tone:** Calm authority — professional friend, not salesperson
2. **Trust signals:** WaterMark / VIC Licensed / 7-Year Warranty (adapted for format)
3. **Core message:** Free test, no obligation, approximately 30 minutes
4. **Appointment duration:** Always "approximately 30 minutes" — never a range
5. **CTA style:** Benefit-led, specific, action verb first
6. **No fabricated proof:** No invented reviews, ratings, or testimonials
7. **Pain-first:** Lead with the problem or context before the solution
8. **Contact:** 03 9122 5243 in every channel where space allows

## Channel 1: Google Ads (Search)

### Constraints
- Headline: 30 characters max (×15 available)
- Description: 90 characters max (×4 available)
- CTA: Set by Google (button label choices only)
- Display URL path: 15 characters per field

### Brand Adaptation Rules
- Match the search query's intent in the first headline
- Include one trust signal in descriptions (WaterMark / Licensed / Warranty)
- Always include "Free" — it's the conversion unlock word
- Never fabricate review counts in ad copy

**Offer headlines:**
- Free Water Test — Melbourne
- Book Your Free Water Test
- Free In-Home Water Test
- No Obligation Water Test

**Trust headlines:**
- WaterMark Certified System
- VIC Licensed & Insured
- 7-Year Workmanship Warranty
- Melbourne's Water Specialists

**Problem/benefit headlines:**
- Every Tap. Every Shower. Clean.
- Chlorine & Sediment Removed
- Hard Water? Fixed in Hours
- Know What's in Your Water

- Whole-Home Filtration System
- Installed Under 2 Hours

### Approved Description Bank

2. "Remove chlorine, sediment & trace metals from every tap. Free in-home test. 7-year workmanship warranty. Melbourne's water specialists."
3. "One system filters every tap and shower. VIC Licensed installation. Free test — 0% interest plans available. Book today."

### Display URL Format
`neptunefiltration.com.au/free-water-test`

### What NOT to Do in Google Ads
- Do not claim "#1" or "best" without substantiation
- Do not invent review counts ("500+ happy customers")
- Do not use exclamation marks in every headline
- Do not use vague CTAs like "Learn More" as primary

## Channel 2: Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

### Constraints
- Primary text: 125 characters visible before "more" on feed
- Headline (link): 40 characters
- Description: 25 characters (often not shown)
- Image: 1200×628px (feed), 1080×1080px (square), 1080×1920px (stories/reels)
- Video: 9:16 (stories/reels), 16:9 or 1:1 (feed)

### Brand Adaptation Rules
- First 125 characters of primary text must hook — the rest is hidden
- Pain-first or curiosity hooks work best for cold audiences
- Use real Melbourne suburbs in copy for local relevance
- All text on image/video must work without sound (captions for video)
- No fabricated review numbers — use credentials instead
- One primary CTA per ad

### Hook Formulas (First 2 Lines)

Problem hook:
```
"Most Melbourne homes have higher chlorine levels than they realise.
Here's what your family is actually absorbing every day…"
```

Curiosity hook:
```
"We tested tap water in 20 Melbourne suburbs last month.
Not one came back perfect. Here's what we found:"
```

Stat hook:
```
"6× more chlorine is absorbed through your skin in a 10-minute shower
than from drinking a glass of tap water."
```

Credibility hook:
```
"WaterMark Certified. VIC Licensed & Insured. 7-Year Warranty.
And a free in-home water test — approximately 30 minutes, no obligation."
```

### Approved Ad Structure

**Cold audience (awareness):**
```
Hook (problem/curiosity) [2 lines]
Context [2–3 lines — Melbourne water, what's in it]
Solution [2 lines — Neptune system, what it removes]
Proof [1–2 lines — certifications, warranty]
CTA [1 line — "Book your free water test →"]
Reassurance ["No obligation. Approximately 30 minutes."]
```

**Warm/retargeting (consideration):**
```
Reminder + offer [2 lines]
Remove remaining objection [2–3 lines]
Trust signals [credentials]
CTA + urgency [if real scarcity exists]
```

### Creative Direction
- Dark hero sections with clear white text overlays
- Real filtration system imagery — not stock
- Real Melbourne interior photography — premium kitchens, bathrooms
- Video: show the test being done, the system being installed, water flowing clean
- Reels: text on screen (no sound assumption), fast-paced, 15–30 seconds

### What NOT to Do in Meta Ads
- No fake countdown timers
- No exaggerated health claims ("cures eczema") — use "customers report improvements"
- No fabricated review counts or screenshots of fake reviews
- No all-caps text in body copy
- No stock photo suburban families (too generic)

## Channel 3: Email

### Technical Standards
- Max width: 600px (renders correctly across clients)
- Background: surface-white (#FFFFFF) or surface-off-white for sections
- Font: system-safe stack or web font with fallback (confirm with email platform)
- Font size: 16px body minimum, 24–32px headlines
- Button: neptune-blue, 16px, 280px wide minimum, 48px height (tappable)
- Mobile: single column, button full-width

### Email Structure Standard

```
HEADER:
  Neptune Filtration logo (centred, max 180px wide)
  Background: neptune-deep or surface-white

BODY:
  Headline (H2 size, text-primary)
  Body copy (16px, 1.65 line height, max 600px width)
  Sections separated by 40–48px gap
  One primary CTA button per email
  CTA placement: after the primary value has been delivered (not first thing)

FOOTER:
  Neptune Filtration | Melbourne, VIC
  03 9122 5243 | office@neptunefiltration.com.au
  WaterMark Certified AS 3497 · VIC Licensed & Insured · 7-Year Workmanship Warranty
  [Unsubscribe link] · [Privacy Policy]
  © 2025 Neptune Filtration
```

### Email Tone by Flow

| Flow | Tone |
|------|------|
| Welcome / Lead nurture | Helpful, educational, warm |
| Pre-appointment | Reassuring, preparatory, calm |
| Post-install | Celebratory, practical, friendly |
| Filter reminder | Brief, helpful, direct |
| Reactivation | Honest, low-pressure, real |

### Subject Line Standards

- Mobile preview: max 41 characters
- Preview text: 90 characters (supports subject)
- Avoid: "URGENT", "ACT NOW", "LIMITED TIME" (looks spammy, misaligned with brand)
- Use: Curiosity, personalisation (first name), specific benefit

Approved subject line formulas:
- "[First name], here's what we found in Melbourne tap water"
- "Your free water test is confirmed ✓"
- "What happens at your Neptune appointment"
- "Time to replace your Neptune filters"
- "One question about your water, [First Name]"

### What NOT to Do in Email
- No image-only emails (blocked by many clients)
- No red urgent CTA buttons (contradicts calm authority brand)
- No fabricated testimonials in emails
- No countdown timers in email unless genuinely real
- No all-caps subject lines

## Channel 4: SMS

### Constraints
- Single segment: 160 characters (aim for this)
- Always identify sender: "Neptune Filtration" or "Neptune"
- Always include opt-out: "Reply STOP to unsubscribe"
- Hours: 8am–8pm AEDT only
- No more than 2 messages per flow without customer response

### SMS Tone
Ultra-brief. One action. Never pushy. No credentials (no room). No brand voice flourishes.

### Approved SMS Templates (confirmed correct)

See [sms/flows/inquiry.md](../sms/flows/inquiry.md) for full flow templates.

**Standard format:**
```
"[Brand ID]: [One action/message]. [Link or phone if needed]. Reply STOP to opt out."
```

**Example:**
```
"Neptune: Your free water test is confirmed. We'll call to confirm your time.
Questions? 03 9122 5243 Reply STOP to opt out."
```

### What NOT to Do in SMS
- No exclamation marks (feels pushy)
- No "URGENT" or "LAST CHANCE" (not the Neptune tone)
- No sending outside 8am–8pm
- No more than 2 unsolicited SMS to a cold lead
- No fabricated urgency ("only 2 slots left!" unless literally true)

## Channel 5: Brochure / Print

### Print Specs
- CMYK colour (not RGB — confirm conversions with printer)
- Neptune Deep (approximate CMYK): C90 M60 Y30 K70
- Neptune Blue (approximate CMYK): C85 M40 Y5 K0
- Font: same as web — confirm embed rights with font licence
- Minimum body text: 9pt (print) / 11pt for high legibility
- Bleed: 3mm on all sides
- Safe zone: 5mm from trim edge

### Brochure Structure (A4, 2-fold)

**Page 1 (cover):**
- Neptune Filtration logo
- Headline: "Clean water. Every tap. Every shower."
- Hero image: premium Melbourne interior
- "Melbourne's Water Specialists."
- Trust bar: WaterMark Certified AS 3497 · VIC Licensed & Insured · 7-Year Warranty

**Page 2–3 (inside):**
- The problem (Melbourne water context)
- The Neptune system (3 stages, what they remove)
- What changes (4 benefit categories)
- Appointment process (3 steps, ~30 minutes, no obligation)

**Page 4 (back):**
- Contact: 03 9122 5243 | office@neptunefiltration.com.au | neptunefiltration.com.au
- CTA: "Book your free water test at neptunefiltration.com.au"
- QR code → links to booking page
- Trust bar repeated
- © Neptune Filtration · Melbourne, VIC · WaterMark Certified

### What NOT to Do in Print
- No small print with hidden conditions
- No fabricated testimonials or review counts
- No stock photos (use real installs / real system imagery)
- No colour mismatches from digital brand (CMYK must be calibrated)

## Channel Governance

Before creating any channel-specific asset:

1. Read the relevant channel section above for constraints
2. Confirm the funnel stage and what action you want the person to take
3. Use the canonical CTA for that stage (from design-system.md)
4. Apply the pain → mechanism → proof → offer → CTA structure
5. Ensure at minimum one trust signal appears (adapted for channel format)
6. Never fabricate reviews, ratings, testimonials, or statistics
7. Verify appointment time is stated as "approximately 30 minutes" wherever duration appears
