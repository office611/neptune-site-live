# Neptune Filtration — Offer Architecture

## Core Philosophy

Every offer must answer: "What do I get, how much does it cost, and why should I act now?"
The free water test is our primary lead magnet. Everything else builds from it.

---

## Offer Stack

### Tier 0 — Lead Magnet (Free, No Risk)
**"Free In-Home Water Test"**
- What they get: Certified tech visits, tests water on-site, full results report
- What it costs: $0
- Time to deliver: [X] business days to schedule
- Why now: "Book this week and we'll test and quote same-day"
- Conversion goal: Book appointment
- Objection handler: "No pressure, no sales pitch — just your water results"

---

### Tier 1 — Entry Product (Low commitment)
**"[Product name] — Under-Sink Filter"**
- What they get: [X]-stage filtration, removes [contaminants list], [X]-year warranty
- Price: $[X] installed
- Monthly: $[X]/month for filter replacement plan
- Guarantee: 90-day satisfaction or full refund
- Ideal for: Renters, first-time buyers, taste/smell issues only

---

### Tier 2 — Core Product (Main revenue driver)
**"[Product name] — Whole-Home Filtration System"**
- What they get: Filters all water in the home, [X]-stage, [X] GPD capacity
- Price: $[X] installed
- Monthly: $[X]/month all-inclusive (maintenance + filters)
- Guarantee: Lifetime service guarantee + [X]-year parts warranty
- Finance: $[X]/month, [X] months, 0% interest
- Ideal for: Families, health-focused buyers, hard water issues

---

### Tier 3 — Premium / Bundle (Highest AOV)
**"Neptune Complete — Whole Home + RO + Softener"**
- What they get: Full home coverage, reverse osmosis drinking water, soft water throughout
- Price: $[X] installed
- Monthly: $[X]/month all-inclusive
- Guarantee: Lifetime service + annual health check visit
- Finance: $[X]/month, [X] months
- Ideal for: Serious buyers, multiple issues, high-end homes

---

## Guarantee Architecture

Stack these in order on every sales page:

1. **Free test guarantee** — "If we can't improve your water quality, we tell you."
2. **Installation guarantee** — "We leave your home exactly as we found it."
3. **Performance guarantee** — "Your water will be [X] or we come back free."
4. **Satisfaction guarantee** — "Not happy in 90 days? Full refund."
5. **Service guarantee** — "Any issue, we're there within [X] hours."

---

## Urgency & Scarcity Triggers

- "We only take [X] new installs per week — [X] spots left this month"
- "Price increase on [date] — lock in today's rate"
- "Free water test slots: [X] remaining this week"
- "This month's install special ends [date]"

Use real scarcity only. Never fabricate.

---

## Upsell Paths (Post-Purchase)

1. Under-sink customer → Whole-home system (show whole-home benefits 30 days post-install)
2. Whole-home → Add RO drinking system (email at 60 days)
3. Any customer → Annual maintenance plan (at 11 months)
4. Any customer → Refer a friend program (at 30 days, when satisfaction is high)
