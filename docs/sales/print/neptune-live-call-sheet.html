Discovery Branches
SPECIFIC (skin / taste / smell) → "Yeah, makes a lot of sense — more common than people realise." → "Most people have been dealing with it a while without connecting it back to the water." → [Move to LOCATE] GENERAL / "just curious" → "That's how most people start — just a feeling the water could be better." → [Move to LOCATE]
Problem Anchor Branches
"Noticed anything yourself?" YES → "Yeah, makes sense — once you understand what's in the water, it clicks." [Move to HABITS] NO → "Fair enough — can be subtle. Or people just get used to it." [Do NOT mention assessment yet. Move to HABITS.]
Partner Check (Before Time Slots)
"Before I lock something in — anyone else who'd normally be across a decision like this?" YES → "Worth having them there — tech gives fixed quote on the spot. Easier when everyone's across it." NO → "No worries — just wanted to check." → [Binary close]
Water Habits Branches
"Tap or bottled?" BOTTLED → A: "Yeah — how long have you been doing that?" B: "Was that more about taste or just a general thing?" C: (if long) "What made you start — was there something specific?" After answer → "Yeah, makes sense." → [Move to TESTED?] TAP → A: "Has anything ever made you think twice about it?" B: "No real issues noticed, or just haven't looked into it?" After answer → "Yeah, gotcha." → [Move to TESTED?] RULE: Ask ONE follow-up. Then move. ≠ expand ≠ explain.
Prior Testing Branches
"Ever had your water properly tested before?" NO (most common) → "Yeah, pretty typical. So you've got a concern, managing it one way or another, but no real picture of what's going on." → [Pause] "That's exactly what we'd look to fix." → [Move to FRAME] YES → "The testing side's similar either way." → "The visit is about seeing your home — plumbing, how you use it, what you're after." → "That's where the right recommendation actually comes from." → [Move to FRAME]
Objection Quick-Ref
PRICE / "GIVE ME A NUMBER"
Power line → plumbing/size differ → tech gives fixed quote on spot → close
"JUST SEND INFO"
Generic = useless for your water → assessment = 30 mins, tells you → close
"ALREADY HAD A TEST"
Testing's similar → visit = seeing home, plumbing, usage, goals → close
"NOT INTERESTED"
No obligation → most start curious → at least know what's in your water → close
"TOO EXPENSIVE"
Exactly why we don't price on phone → free assessment → know first → close
"NEED TO THINK ABOUT IT"
What specifically? → address it → free, worst case = you know more → close
Still unsure: "Can I check back [Day]?"
"I'M RENTING"
Owner-occupied only → looking to buy? → add to list if yes → warm close
"GETTING OTHER QUOTES"
Makes sense → get our tech out → compare apples to apples → close
