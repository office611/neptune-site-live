## SECTION-BY-SECTION REFERENCE

> Read the INTENT column, not the lines. The lines are reminders — your own words are fine.

---

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OPEN | Sound human | *"quick chat / best way to help"* | ↑ smile |

## WATER HABITS BRANCHES

```
"Tap or bottled?"

BOTTLED  →  Option A: "Yeah — how long have you been doing that?"
            Option B: "Was that more about taste or just a general thing?"
            After answer → "Yeah, makes sense." → [Move to TESTED?]

TAP      →  Option A: "Has anything ever made you think twice about it?"
            Option B: "No real issues noticed, or just haven't looked into it?"
            After answer → "Yeah, gotcha." → [Move to TESTED?]

RULE: Ask ONE follow-up. Then move. ≠ expand ≠ explain.
```

## OBJECTION QUICK-REF

> Strategy keywords only. Do not read. Glance and respond naturally.

---

**PRICE / "JUST GIVE ME A NUMBER"**
Power line → your plumbing / home size differ → tech gives fixed quote on spot → close

**"JUST SEND INFO"**
Generic info = useless for your water → assessment = 30 mins, actually tells you → close

**"ALREADY HAD A TEST"**
Testing's similar → visit is about seeing the home, plumbing, usage, goals → close

**"NOT INTERESTED / JUST CURIOUS"**
No obligation → most people start curious → at least know what's in your water → close

**"TOO EXPENSIVE"**
Exactly why we don't price on phone → free assessment → know what you're dealing with first → close

**"NEED TO THINK ABOUT IT"**
What specifically are you weighing up? → address it → free, low-pressure, worst case = you know more → close
If still unsure: "Can I check back [Day]?"

**"PARTNER NEEDS TO BE THERE"**
Not hard requirement → saves relaying everything → tech gives quote on spot → smoother together → continue

**"I'M RENTING"**
Needs to be owner-occupied → looking to buy? → if yes: add to list, follow up later → warm close

**"SEEN A COMPETITOR / GETTING QUOTES"**
Makes sense — big decision → get our tech out, compare apples to apples → we let the assessment speak → close

## TONALITY SYMBOLS (Quick reference)

| ↓ pace | slow down — problem anchor, assessment frame |
|---|---|
| → wait | don't fill silence — after close, after questions |
| ↑ smile | lift tone — opening, close out |
| ≠ expand | don't add more — after habits responses |
| ? only | ask a question, don't explain — habits section |
| one beat | pause after line — after anchor, after no-decision line |
