What's in your water.
Melbourne Water treats your supply to meet the Australian Drinking Water Guidelines. Meeting the guideline is the legal minimum — not the standard you'd choose for your household.
01
Chlorine is present in every home.
Added at high levels to disinfect the distribution network. Active in your drinking water, cooking water, and every shower — every day. Does not evaporate at room temperature.
02
Scale and sediment vary by suburb.
Calcium and magnesium accumulate on shower screens, taps, and appliances. Just 1mm of scale reduces water heater efficiency by up to 11%. Your test reflects your specific supply.
03
Older pipes carry additional risk.
Lead and mercury leach from pre-1970 infrastructure after leaving treatment. Tasteless, odourless, and untraceable without testing. Common in established Melbourne suburbs.
Neptune 5·1·1 Micron — The 3-Stage System
STAGE 01 5 Micron
Pleated Sediment Filter
Removes
Rust & sediment Pipe particles Physical debris Coarse suspended matter
A human hair is 70 microns. Stage 01 catches particles 14× smaller.
Mechanical depth filtration. Pleated design maximises surface area, extending filter life and trapping efficiency between annual services.
STAGE 02 1 Micron
Scale Reduction Carbon Block
Removes
Chlorine taste & odour Calcium & magnesium Scale-forming minerals Hardness deposits
Activated carbon adsorption binds chlorine and mineral ions. Scale-reduction media specifically targets calcium and magnesium — the primary cause of limescale on fittings and appliances.
STAGE 03 1 Micron
Coconut Carbon Block
Removes
Pesticides & herbicides VOCs (volatile organics) Lead & mercury Disinfection by-products Residual chloramine
Coconut shell carbon has up to 1,200 m² of surface area per gram — more than a football field, compressed into one filter.
Coconut shell carbon's highly porous microstructure captures organic compounds, heavy metals, and chlorine reaction products that standard carbon cannot address at 1 micron.
