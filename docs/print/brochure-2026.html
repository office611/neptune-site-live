Melbourne water
contaminants.

Melbourne's water supply is treated and generally meets Australian standards — but treated water is not filtered water. Treatment adds chlorine. Pipe infrastructure adds sediment. Your suburb's geology determines your scale load.

Chlorine & Chloramines
Present · All suburbs
Added during treatment and remains active in your supply at the tap. Absorbed through skin in the shower, tasted in drinking water, and implicated in dry skin and hair. Chloramines (monochloramine) are increasingly used and are harder to remove than free chlorine — requiring activated carbon filtration, not basic filters.
Hardness / Calcium & Magnesium
Variable · Suburb-dependent
Hard water minerals cause visible scale buildup on shower screens, taps, kettles, and appliances — a daily reality for many Melbourne homeowners. The extent varies by suburb, with some areas running significantly harder than others. Scale accelerates wear on hot water systems and dishwashers over time.
Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs)
Trace levels · Pipe & industrial source
VOCs — including trihalomethanes (THMs) formed when chlorine reacts with organic matter — are present in treated reticulated supplies. They evaporate readily during showers. Removal requires dedicated coconut shell activated carbon, not sediment filtration alone.
Heavy Metals — Lead & Mercury
Trace · Older infrastructure
Lead enters water through older service connections and internal plumbing — particularly in homes built before the 1980s. There is no safe level of lead in drinking water for children. Reduction is supported through Neptune's dedicated coconut shell activated carbon stage, engineered for treated metropolitan water conditions.
Sediment, Rust & Particulates
Present · All properties
Pipe scale and fine particulates enter through your supply line and internal plumbing. Visible as discolouration or fine particles in water. First point of capture in a staged filtration system — essential before carbon stages.

Why coconut shell carbon matters.
Coconut shell activated carbon is produced at higher activation temperatures, yielding a microporous structure with surface area exceeding 1,000 m²/g. Coal-based carbon — standard in low-cost filters — has significantly lower micropore volume and reduced adsorption capacity for VOCs, chloramines, and heavy metals. The Neptune Stage 3 filter is specified to perform where generic carbon cannot.
