## Format Note

6 content pages in print is non-standard for saddle stitch (which requires multiples of 4).
**Recommended physical format:** A single sheet folded in thirds — gatefold or Z-fold —
gives 6 panels across both sides. At 210×210mm per panel with premium uncoated stock,
this feels considered and unusual. Most plumbing companies don't print this format.
That gap is the point.

Alternatively: 8-page saddle-stitched booklet where the opening spread (pages 2–3)
functions as one visual moment — effectively 6 content positions. Discuss with printer.

## PAGE 1 — COVER

**Purpose:** First impression. The homeowner sees this before the consultant speaks.
It must communicate serious, premium company immediately.

**Content:**
- Full-bleed installation photography
- NEPTUNE wordmark — white, lower-left or lower-centre
- "Whole Home Filtration" — beneath wordmark, light weight
- Nothing else

**Photography:** Neptune unit in premium Melbourne garden. Golden-hour light.
Copper pipes visible. Dark foliage framing. Clean, architectural, warm.

**Consultant use:** Place on the table as you arrive. The cover does its work
without narration.

---

## PAGE 2 — WHAT'S IN YOUR WATER

**Purpose:** After the water test results are discussed, this page validates what
the homeowner just learned and gives it context. The consultant points to each
block — the page does the explaining, not a verbal paragraph.

**Headline:** What's in your water.

**Three anchor blocks:**

**Chlorine is present in every Melbourne home.**
Used in the treatment process and active in your supply. You cook with it,
shower in it, and absorb it through your skin every morning.

**Scale and sediment vary by suburb.**
Hard water minerals cause scale on taps, shower screens, and appliances.
The level depends on your area — your test results reflect your specific supply.

**Stat callout (visually separated, copper accent, larger type):**
6× more chlorine is absorbed through skin in a 10-minute shower
than from drinking a glass of tap water.

**Layout:** Three stacked blocks, left-aligned. Clean margins.
The 6× stat sits apart — visually distinct, designed to stop the eye.
Minimal or no photography on this page. Let the facts carry it.

**Consultant use:** "Here's why what we found in your test matters."
Point to each block in sequence. Pause on the stat. That number needs a moment.

---

## PAGE 3 — THE NEPTUNE SYSTEM

**Purpose:** Introduce the product with confidence and restraint.
The homeowner does not need to understand the technology.
They need to understand the scope and permanence of what they're considering.

**Headline:** The Neptune system.

**Layout:** Photography occupies 55–60% of the page — the Neptune unit installed
in a premium garden setting, copper pipes prominent. Four anchor phrases run
alongside or below the image, each separated by a thin copper rule.

**Anchor phrases:**
Installed at your mains.
Three-stage filtration.
Every tap. Every shower.
Under 2 hours to install.

**Spec line (8pt, text-muted, bottom of page):**
Neptune 5-1-1 Micron System · 20" triple housing · Annual filter service: $299.95

**Consultant use:** "This is the Neptune system." Point to the image.
Walk through the four phrases. The homeowner looks at the unit while you speak.

---

## PAGE 4 — WHAT YOU NOTICE

**Purpose:** Translate filtration into lived experience. This is the emotional
centre of the brochure. The homeowner imagines their home after installation.
Not features — daily reality.

**Headline:** What you notice.

**Layout:**
Left half or top: lifestyle photography — premium shower scene, kitchen detail,
or water flowing from a beautiful tap. Clean, aspirational, atmospheric.

Right half or below: Five benefit statements, each on its own line,
thin copper rules between them. No paragraphs. No bullet points.

**Benefit statements:**
Your shower no longer smells of chlorine.
Skin that was dry becomes noticeably less dry.
Scale stops accumulating on glass, taps, and shower screens.
Every tap delivers filtered water. Not just one.
Your appliances run cleaner.

**Consultant use:** "Let me show you what actually changes." Pause on the photography.
Then point to each benefit line. Let the homeowner read ahead — that is the brochure working.

---

## PAGE 5 — PROCESS + CREDENTIALS

**Purpose:** Remove friction and establish trust in one page.
The homeowner has two remaining questions: what does proceeding involve,
and why should I trust this company? This page answers both.

**Layout:**
Upper section: Process — three numbered steps
Lower section: Credential strip — dark band (Neptune Deep), white text, three credentials

**Headline:** One appointment. Done properly.

**Process steps:**

01 — Free water test. Results on the day.
We test your actual tap water and walk you through what we find. ~30 minutes.

02 — Installation. Under 2 hours.
Our VIC Licensed plumbers install the system at your mains connection.
We leave the site clean.

03 — Annual filter service.
We return once a year to replace your filters. $299.95.

**Credential strip (dark Neptune Deep background, white text, full width):**
WaterMark Certified AS 3497 · VIC Licensed & Insured · 7-Year Workmanship Warranty

**Consultant use:**
Process: "Here's exactly what happens." Walk through steps 01–03.
Credentials: Point to the strip. "This is who backs us up."
The strip is also the objection-handling anchor — flip here directly if a trust
question surfaces on any other page.

---

## PAGE 6 — BACK COVER

**Purpose:** Brand close. Left with the homeowner after the appointment.
Calm, confident, complete.

**Content:**
- Full-bleed Neptune Deep background or dark photography
- NEPTUNE wordmark — white, centred
- "Whole Home Filtration" — beneath wordmark, light weight

# DESIGN SYSTEM

## Colours
- Neptune Deep: #0B1E3D — headlines, dark sections, credential strip
- White: #FFFFFF — text on dark, wordmark
- Copper: #B87333 — rules, step numbers, stat callout accent
- Text secondary: #4A5C72 — process step descriptions
- Text muted: #7A8EA3 — spec line

## Typography (Poppins throughout)
| Role | Size | Weight |
|------|------|--------|
| Page headline | 26–30pt | 700 |
| Anchor phrases | 11–12pt | 600 |
| Stat callout | 14–16pt | 600 |
| Step titles | 10pt | 600 |
| Step descriptions | 9.5pt | 400 |
| Spec line | 8pt | 400 |
| Credential strip | 9pt | 500 |

## Spacing
- Margins: 18mm minimum all sides
- Between content blocks: 10–14mm
- Between steps: 12mm
- Credential strip: full-width, 12mm vertical padding

## Photography
- Colour grade: warm-neutral, highlights lifted, saturation -10 to -15%
- Bleed to edge on photography pages
- No text overlays on photography
- Style: editorial warmth — not stock, not clinical

## Copper rule usage
One copper element per page maximum:
- Page 2: Stat callout accent
- Page 3: Rules between anchor phrases
- Page 4: Rules between benefit lines
- Page 5: Step numbers

## Cover and back cover
Same tonal treatment — dark, architectural, clean. The brochure feels
intentionally circular: it opens and closes with the same emotional register.

## Physical production
| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Cover / panel stock | 350gsm — soft-touch matte laminate + spot UV on wordmark |
| Inner stock | 150gsm uncoated |
| Format | Gatefold or Z-fold (single sheet, 6 panels) — or 8-page saddle stitch |
| Corner treatment | 6mm round-corner trim |
| Print quantity | 50–100 initial run |

# CONSULTANT USE — SUMMARY

**Page sequence during consultation:**

1. Place brochure on table — cover visible. No narration needed.
2. Open to page 2 after water test results. "Here's why this matters."
3. Turn to page 3. "This is the Neptune system."
4. Turn to page 4. Pause on photography. Then walk benefit lines.
5. Turn to page 5. "Here's what happens. Here's who we are."
6. Leave brochure with homeowner. Back cover faces up.

**Objection navigation:**
Any trust question → page 5 credential strip, immediately.
Any process question → page 5 steps 01–03.

**What the consultant does not do:**
Read the copy aloud verbatim.
Rush through pages to reach a close.
Use the brochure to ask for a decision.

The brochure supports the conversation. The consultant closes it.
