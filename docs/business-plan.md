## 1. Executive Summary

Neptune Filtration is a premium residential water filtration company based in Melbourne, Victoria. The business delivers whole-home water filtration systems direct to homeowners across greater Melbourne, combining high-quality product supply with professional installation through a network of licensed subcontracted plumbing teams.

The company was founded by an operator with deep, demonstrable experience in the water filtration industry — including over AUD $800,000 in revenue generated within a 12-month period in a previous venture in the same sector, and a track record of over 100 successfully processed consumer finance applications through Brighte.

Despite being in its early weeks of trading, Neptune Filtration has established immediate commercial traction:

- **7 confirmed sales** in the first week of operations
- **90+ qualified inbound enquiries** generated within a recent two-week period
- **~10 installations per week** currently being delivered
- **Active lead pipeline** driven by Meta advertising and direct-response digital campaigns
- **Expanded operational infrastructure** including 3 Melbourne warehouse locations and 30+ operational and installation staff

This business plan has been prepared to support Neptune Filtration's application to become an approved merchant partner with Elevant, with the objective of embedding integrated consumer finance solutions into its standard sales and customer experience process.

Customer finance is a natural fit for the Neptune Filtration offering. The average system investment sits between AUD $2,500 and $5,000, a price point where a significant proportion of homeowners express strong interest in structured payment options. Offering accessible, interest-free finance through Elevant is expected to improve conversion rates, increase accessibility for a broader range of homeowners, and reduce the friction that would otherwise cause qualified prospects to delay or abandon their purchase decision.

Neptune Filtration's growth target is to scale toward approximately 30 installations per week by the end of the year, generating projected weekly revenue exceeding AUD $100,000. This growth will be supported by increased marketing investment, continued expansion of the installation network, and the accelerating demand for residential water filtration across Melbourne's homeowner market.

## 3. Industry Opportunity

### The Australian Residential Water Filtration Market

Demand for whole-home water filtration in Australia has grown materially over the past several years, driven by a combination of heightened consumer health awareness, growing media coverage of tap water contaminants, and a broader trend toward premium home improvement and wellness investment.

Key market drivers include:

**Health and wellness spending:** Australian households are increasingly investing in the quality of their living environment. Residential water filtration fits squarely within the broader home wellness category — a segment that has experienced consistent growth alongside home improvement spending.

**PFAS and contaminant awareness:** Growing public awareness of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) and other contaminants in municipal water supplies has meaningfully increased consumer interest in point-of-entry filtration. Media coverage of water quality concerns in metropolitan areas has directly accelerated consumer enquiry volumes across the sector.

**Home improvement investment post-COVID:** Australian homeowners have continued to invest in home upgrades at elevated rates. Whole-home filtration is perceived by many homeowners as a structural improvement — adding comfort, health benefit, and long-term value to the property.

**Alternative to single-use plastic:** The environmental cost of bottled water consumption resonates strongly with Australian households. Whole-home filtration positions as a financially and environmentally superior long-term solution.

**Melbourne's specific demand profile:** Greater Melbourne's growing population of homeowners — particularly in suburban and outer-suburban growth corridors — represents a strong addressable market. The combination of new home builds and established homeowners upgrading existing properties creates a dual demand stream.

### Why Now is the Right Time

The timing of Neptune Filtration's launch is deliberate. Consumer awareness of water quality is at an all-time high, and the market for premium, professionally installed home filtration systems is underserved by sophisticated, direct-to-consumer operators in Melbourne. The company is positioned to capture significant market share through superior lead generation, professional customer experience, and finance-enabled accessibility.

## 4. Products & Services

Neptune Filtration supplies and installs premium whole-home water filtration systems for residential properties across greater Melbourne. The core offering is a professionally installed, point-of-entry filtration system designed to treat all water entering the home.

### Core Product: Whole-Home Water Filtration System

Whole-home systems are installed at the mains water entry point of the property, meaning every tap, shower, appliance and fixture in the home receives filtered water. This is a materially different and superior offering to under-sink or benchtop filters, which treat only one outlet.

Key features of the Neptune Filtration system:

- Multi-stage filtration technology addressing sediment, chlorine, heavy metals, and chemical contaminants
- Designed for Australian municipal water conditions
- Long service life with scheduled filter replacement
- Compatible with all residential plumbing configurations
- Professional installation by licensed plumbers with full compliance to Australian standards
- Post-installation system check and customer walkthrough included

## 10. Finance Integration Strategy

### The Role of Finance in Neptune Filtration's Business

Consumer finance is not a peripheral feature of Neptune Filtration's commercial model — it is a structural component of the customer experience and a core driver of conversion performance.

The whole-home filtration category sits in a pricing band where consumer finance has the most pronounced impact on purchase behaviour. A system investment of AUD $3,000–$4,500 is meaningful but not prohibitive. Customers at this price point are typically motivated and ready to buy — but many will pause, delay, or ultimately not proceed when faced with a single upfront payment. Introducing a structured repayment option removes that barrier entirely.

### Why Finance Improves Conversion

**Reframing the purchase decision:** A customer considering a AUD $3,500 system experiences that number very differently as "AUD $42 per fortnight for 36 months." The weekly or fortnightly payment is comfortably within the household budget of most homeowner demographics, whereas the lump sum triggers a more cautious financial evaluation. Finance converts a considered purchase into an accessible one.

**Reducing drop-off at the proposal stage:** Without a finance option, a meaningful proportion of qualified, motivated customers will request time to "think about it" or "speak to their partner." In practice, this often means the sale is lost. With finance presented confidently at the consultation stage, the decision becomes immediately actionable.

**Increasing average order value:** Customers purchasing on finance are more likely to select higher-specification systems or upgrade packages, because the incremental repayment difference between a standard and premium system is modest. A customer deciding between a AUD $3,200 and AUD $4,200 system will be significantly more influenced by an extra AUD $9 per fortnight than by an extra AUD $1,000 upfront.

**Improving sales team efficiency:** A structured finance offer gives the sales team a clear, repeatable close tool. Rather than managing complex price negotiation, the consultation focuses on product fit and monthly affordability — a simpler, faster, and more customer-friendly conversation.

### Finance Utilisation Assumptions

| Metric | Estimate |
|---|---|
| **Customers requesting finance options** | 60–70% at consultation stage |
| **Expected finance approval rate** | 75–85% of applicants |
| **Average financed amount** | AUD $2,500 – $3,500 |
| **Expected finance utilisation rate (of all sales)** | 50–65% |

At 10 installations per week with a 55% finance utilisation rate and AUD $3,000 average financed amount:
- **~6 financed transactions per week**
- **~AUD $18,000 in financed volume per week**
- **~AUD $75,000–$80,000 in monthly financed volume**

At the 30-installations-per-week target:
- **~18 financed transactions per week**
- **~AUD $54,000 in financed volume per week**
- **~AUD $230,000+ in monthly financed volume**

### Responsible Finance Management

Neptune Filtration is committed to responsible sales practices in the context of consumer finance. This commitment is not only an ethical position — it is a business necessity. Customers who enter finance agreements they cannot manage create downstream problems for all parties. The approach taken by the business reflects the founder's hands-on experience managing 100+ finance applications:

- Finance is presented clearly, with repayment terms, interest-free periods, and total cost of credit explained transparently
- Sales staff are trained to assess customer suitability before progressing a finance application, rather than maximising application volume at the expense of quality
- Customers are not pushed toward finance if cash payment is their preference
- Applications are submitted accurately and completely, with no coaching of customers to misrepresent their financial position
- Post-installation, customers receive clear written documentation of their repayment schedule and how to manage their account

This approach protects the quality of Neptune Filtration's finance book and maintains the business's standing as a responsible merchant partner.
