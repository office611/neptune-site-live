# Neptune Filtration — Audience & Personas

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Primary:** Homeowner, 32–58, owns their home, has children or elderly parents,
recently moved OR just noticed a water issue (smell, taste, staining).
Motivated by health and family safety, not by saving money.

**Secondary:** Small business owner (restaurant, office, gym) who needs reliable
water quality for customers or staff.

---

## Personas

### Persona 1: "Sarah the Health-Conscious Mum"
- **Age:** 35–48
- **Situation:** Has young kids, reads labels, buys organic, worried about PFAS/chlorine
- **Trigger:** Saw news story about local water quality OR child complained about water taste
- **Pain:** Doesn't know what's actually in her water. Buying bottled water feels wasteful.
- **Objection:** "Is this really necessary? How do I know it works?"
- **What she needs to hear:** "We test your water first so you know exactly what you're dealing with."
- **Best channel:** Facebook/Instagram, Google search, email nurture
- **CTA that works:** "Get a free water test" (low commitment, high value)

### Persona 2: "Mark the Property Owner"
- **Age:** 40–60
- **Situation:** Owns home 5+ years. Noticing scale buildup, rust stains, bad taste.
- **Trigger:** Plumber mentioned hard water. Appliances failing early.
- **Pain:** Replacing appliances is expensive. Scale is damaging pipes.
- **Objection:** "Will this actually pay for itself?"
- **What he needs to hear:** "Our systems extend appliance life by [X] years and pay for themselves in [X] months."
- **Best channel:** Google search ("water softener [city]"), YouTube ads
- **CTA that works:** "Get a free system quote" or "See how much you'll save"

### Persona 3: "New Homeowner"
- **Age:** 28–40
- **Situation:** Just bought a home, hasn't tested water yet
- **Trigger:** Moving in, setting up home, thinking about safety
- **Pain:** Doesn't know what the previous owners dealt with. Wants to start fresh.
- **Objection:** "I just moved in — I don't want another big expense"
- **What they need to hear:** "Start with a free test. No obligation. Know what you're working with."
- **Best channel:** Google (intent-based), Meta retargeting after website visit
- **CTA that works:** "Free water test for new homeowners"

## Pain Points (Ranked by Conversion Power)

1. **Health & safety** — "I don't know what my family is drinking" [highest converter]
2. **Taste & smell** — "Our water smells like chlorine / rotten eggs"
3. **Scale & staining** — "Hard water is destroying our appliances / shower"
4. **Cost of bottled water** — "We spend $X/month on bottled water"
5. **Skin & hair** — "Hard water ruins my hair and dries my skin"

## Customer Journey

```
AWARE → Sees ad / search result / word of mouth
  ↓
INTEREST → Clicks, lands on page, reads about water quality
  ↓
CONSIDERATION → Books free water test or requests quote
  ↓
INTENT → Attends appointment, sees test results
  ↓
PURCHASE → Chooses system, books install
  ↓
LOYALTY → Works perfectly, tells neighbours, subscribes to filter changes
  ↓
ADVOCACY → Leaves review, refers friends
```
