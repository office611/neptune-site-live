## Local Landing Page Structure

Create a page for each major suburb/area served.

**URL pattern:** neptunefiltration.com.au/water-filtration-[suburb]

**Page template:**
- H1: "Water Filtration in [Suburb] — Free In-Home Test"
- 2-sentence intro with suburb name and pain point
- How it works (3 steps)
- Local reviews (filter to show [suburb] reviews if possible)
- FAQ specific to local water quality issues
- CTA: Book free test

**Content differentiation between pages:**
- Mention local water authority / source
- Reference any known local water quality issues
- Include suburb-specific review quotes

## Review Acquisition System

**Target:** 5+ new reviews per month on Google

**Timing:** Request at 2 moments of peak satisfaction:
1. Immediately after appointment (tech gets live approval)
2. 7 days post-install (SMS + email)

**Script for tech (in-person):**
> "Before I go — are you happy with everything today?
> [If yes] Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It takes 60 seconds
> and really helps us. I can text you the link right now."

**QR code:** Print Neptune Google review QR code on invoice and business card.

**Responding to reviews:**
- Reply to every review within 24 hours
- Positive: Thank by name, mention service type, invite them back
- Negative: Acknowledge, apologise, offer to resolve offline (never argue publicly)

## Technical SEO Checklist

- [ ] Page speed: <2.5s LCP on mobile (use PageSpeed Insights)
- [ ] Mobile-first: Test every page on iOS and Android
- [ ] Schema markup: LocalBusiness, Service, Review, FAQ
- [ ] NAP consistency: Name, Address, Phone identical across all directories
- [ ] Sitemaps: Submit XML sitemap to Google Search Console
- [ ] Core Web Vitals: Monitor monthly in Search Console
- [ ] Internal linking: Every blog post links to the free test booking page
