# Blog Template — Water Quality Article

## Template: Problem-Aware Article

Use for: "Why does my water smell/taste/look X" type searches

---

**[Title]:** "Why Does My Tap Water [PROBLEM]? (And How to Fix It)"

**Meta description:** "If your tap water [problem], here's what's causing it —
and the fastest way to fix it. [City] homeowners: [X] options explained."

---

**Intro (100–150 words)**

Opening hook: State the problem plainly. Empathise. Promise the answer.

> "If you've noticed [problem], you're not imagining it — and you're not alone.
> This is one of the most common water complaints we hear from [City] homeowners.
> The good news: it's almost always fixable. The better news: knowing the cause
> makes the fix much simpler (and cheaper) than you'd expect.
> Here's what's actually going on — and what you can do about it."

---

**H2: What Causes [Problem]?**

List 3–5 causes in order of likelihood. For each:
- One-sentence explanation (plain English)
- How to confirm this is the cause
- Severity: Low / Medium / High

---

**H2: Is It Dangerous?**

Honest, balanced answer. Don't over-alarm. Don't dismiss.
Reference relevant health guidelines or standards if applicable.
Include a note about testing: "The only way to know for sure is to test your water."

---

**H2: How to Fix [Problem]**

**Option 1: DIY (low cost, partial fix)**
- What it involves
- What it doesn't fix
- Cost estimate

**Option 2: Point-of-use filter (medium cost)**
- What it is
- What it fixes
- Best for: [situations]

**Option 3: Whole-home filtration (complete fix)**
- What it covers
- Why it's the permanent solution
- Best for: [situations]

CTA block:
> **Not sure which option is right for you?**
> We offer a free in-home water test for [City] homeowners.
> We test, we report, we recommend — zero cost, zero obligation.

---

**H2: Frequently Asked Questions**

3–5 short Q&A covering related searches. Use exact question phrasing from Google's "People Also Ask."

---

**Conclusion (50–75 words)**

Summarise the key takeaway. Reinforce that testing is the first step. Repeat CTA.

> "The [problem] in your water is fixable. The right solution depends on what's causing it —
> and the only way to know that for certain is a water test. If you're in [City],
> ours is completely free. Book below and we'll have your results within the hour."

## Article Length Guide

| Intent | Recommended Length |
|--------|-------------------|
| Problem-aware (what is X) | 800–1200 words |
| Educational (how does X work) | 1000–1500 words |
| Comparison (X vs Y) | 1000–1500 words |
| Pillar / guide | 1800–2500 words |
